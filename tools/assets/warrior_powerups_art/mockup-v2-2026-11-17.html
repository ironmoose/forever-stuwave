GUNSIGHT WARRIOR: PROC ALERTS v2

Design mockup, 2026-11-17, Overpower and Revenge rebuilt with generated pixel art (v1: 2026-11-13)

The two Warrior procs brought up to the Druid Clearcasting standard: the art is generated (ComfyUI mascot, pixelized, cleaned by hand) and every animation is built from the art's own pixels. Overpower is the double axe (candidate 2, without its flame swipe) in front of a cyan rune circle (the idea of candidate 7). Revenge is the cackling Viking skull (candidate 2) on a round Viking shield, because Revenge comes from a block, dodge or parry. The titles, timings, drain, cast and expiry flows are the approved v1 ones. Each column loops: entrance, held with the drain (amber in the last 1.5 s), then the exit; odd loops end in a cast, even loops in expiry.

Off switch: Warrior options, "Power-up alert", on or off, default on.

OVERPOWER

live capture, alert centred on the character
The double axe and the rune circle. The rune circle draws itself (two rings traced round from the top) and its 16 Elder Futhark runes light one by one, while the double axe chops straight down from above in 3 ghost frames with cyan speed lines, bites into the heart of the circle and the screen shakes in whole pixel steps: white flash, a white ring snaps round the rune circle, pink ring, 16 sparks. Held: the circle turns one rune slot every 0.35 s and a white pulse runs round it, a glint walks down each cyan blade edge, embers rise. Amber: runes and blade edges amber. Cast: the axe rips up and away, a white and cyan shock ring rolls out, speed streaks trail the axe and the runes fly outward. Expiry: the runes go dark one by one, the axe goes grey, the title sinks and fades.

REVENGE

live capture, alert centred on the character
The cackling skull on a shield. A round Viking shield (painted planks, a pink and cyan split, iron rim and rivets) slams in from the side in 3 steps and rings with a white shock ring and sparks, then the skull rises up from behind it with a pink flash. The cackle is built from the skull's own pixels: the jaw and beard drop 2 or 4 px (the braids stay put) over a dark throat with a pink glow and the head tilts back, three cackles on the entrance and a burst of four every 1.8 s, with HA bits and laugh lines. The eyes flip pink and cyan on each beat, the shield rim flashes and its cracks glow pink. Cast: one last wide cackle, a white flash, the shield splits in two and slides apart, a ring of HA bits flies out and the letters ricochet off. Expiry: the jaw shuts, the eyes go dark, skull and shield grey out and fade.

VICTORY RUSH

live capture, alert centred on the character
Crossed bearded axes on the red sunburst. Generated art split into three layers (the sunburst, each axe) with the flames pulled out as pixels. The sunburst irises open with a white rim, the two axes slide in from the sides with ghost frames and clang into the X: white flash, pink ring, sparks, shake. The flames light from the bottom up, then dance (the colours roll upward, the tips flicker and lick). Held: a light wedge sweeps round the sunburst, the axes kick apart and clang back every 2.4 s, a green heal pulse rolls out and plus signs drift up. Amber: the flames burn white hot amber. Cast: the axes fly apart up and out with streaks, a green and white heal ring and 12 plus signs burst out, the letters zip off. Expiry: the flames burn out from the top into smoke, the axes sag and everything greys out.

2 // NOTES

  • Colours: cyan is the power accent (window open), amber the last 1.5 s, pink the cast snap, rage red the helm and word shadow. Same tokens as the Warrior module. Victory Rush adds heal green. Norse motifs: bearded axe, horned helms, braided beards, Elder Futhark runes (Tiwaz, Algiz), Valhalla rays.
  • Build: all art is new and drawn on a 150 x 96 pixel grid shown at 2x: bake each state to a sprite frame (axe, laughing skull with 4 jaw and tilt frames, warrior bust with 3 mouth sizes and an arm lowered 0 to 1, crack rays, HA bits, plus sign, Tiwaz and Algiz runes, 5 x 7 carved title letters in 3 tones) and drive them with alpha steps, integer scale steps (the title letter flip is a width scale) and position steps. A 1 px scanline pass darkens every second row of the art. Flat textures only: no shaders, no rotation, no blur. The title is one bitmap strip drawn as per-letter textures; the diagonal cut, the flip and the rise are texcoord swaps or half offsets.
  • Placement: centred on the character (x 780 in the 1542 px capture), bottom edge about 25 px above the head, so it fills the gap between the two rails. The art is at most 375 px wide in capture terms, so it stays about 50 px clear of the target box (x 980 to 1150, y 420 to 515) and of both rails. The background is a real capture of the Gunsight with a target; the crop here is 0.8 of the capture.
  • Words: OVERPOWER (cleaved in), REVENGE (bounces in with the laugh), VICTORY RUSH (rises with the war cry). The longest title is 134 grid px, about 335 px wide in the capture, so it stays clear of the target box.
