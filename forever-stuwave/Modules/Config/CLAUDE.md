# Config

The `/fsconfig` window: a category nav and a content well, built from `FS.ConfigWindow.UI` builders.

## Key Files
- `ConfigWindow.lua` -- window chrome, the builders, and the Unit Frames, Gunsight HUD, Profiles and Help pages
- `HelpModules.lua` -- the class modules explained: pictures, pins, words and the shared `Layout` (Help page, Gunsight tip, listing)
- `HelpPicturesArt.lua` -- GENERATED by `tools/assets/help_pictures.py`: where each picture sits in `help_pictures.tga`

## Patterns
- A builder takes `(content, opts)`, stacks itself below the previous row, binds `key` to `FS.Config` (or `get`/`set`), registers one refresh with `Track`, and returns its row; `row.control` is the clickable part.
- Pages build lazily on first show and read other modules' `SETTINGS` at build time (`FS.GunsightBoxes`), so a module that is absent just leaves its group out.
- Labels and values only on a row; any explanation goes in the `tip` (a `?` icon tooltip). Player-facing text says "cast bar", never "tape".
- The Help page is the one exception: the commands, then `UI.HelpModules`, a row of class tabs (opens on the player's class, or a Druid's current form; views, so they work read-only), a second tab row for Druid forms and Mage specs, the module picture (`help_pictures.tga`, NEAREST, from `HelpPicturesArt.lua`) with pink numbered pins and cyan arrows, then the numbered lines. Everything about it (pictures, pins, words, `Layout`) lives in `HelpModules.lua`, the one source `tools/assets/help_pictures.py` also runs to bake the atlas and render the same pictures for the CurseForge listing. `UI.HelpModules` must be the last row on its page, since it resizes with the class shown. `FS.ClassModuleHelp[token]` (`HelpModules.Tip`) is the Gunsight HUD page's class module `?` tip, so one edit updates all three. New pictures: add a frame to `tools/assets/help_frames.py`, rerun it and `help_pictures.py`; `tools/helppage-harness.py` checks the bake and every pin.
- `UI.Dropdown` shows `tostring(get())` on its button unless it gets `text(value)`; the Gunsight page's area dropdowns use it to show module names for ids. Its Target box tags group reads `FS.GunsightTags.SETTINGS` and is left out when that is absent.
- Every page is a clipped `ScrollFrame` (`Select` makes it; builders stack into its `fsHolder`). Content past the 432 px content area gives a scroll range, the wheel and a thumb in the well's right margin move it (a click on the rail jumps the thumb there; the thumb and rail hit areas are wider than their 4 px look), `Select` resets it to the top only when the category changes, and a page that fits shows no bar. A page measures itself in `Select` and when the window is shown; call `CW.RefreshScroll()` after adding rows to a built page. On a scrolling page the wheel over a `UI.Slider` (found by walking up to the nearest scroll page) scrolls the page; Shift adjusts the slider. Popups (dropdown menu, dialog) are parented outside the clip; the menu's catcher swallows the wheel and any scroll that moves the page closes the menu. Window, well and content insets are the shared constants at the top of `ConfigWindow.lua` that both the range math and `BuildChrome` use.
- `UI.Toggle` takes an optional `reload` function (master rows): the line it returns shows beside a Reload button on the row, and nil hides both. The Gunsight HUD master switch passes `FS.Gunsight.NeedsReload`, because the switch itself applies live and only a Gunsight that was off at login or the classic combat HUD need `/reload`.
- `UI.Slider` is built from Theme pieces on a plain Button, not a Blizzard Slider. It drags through an `OnUpdate` that exists only while the mouse is down.

## Dependencies
- Depends on: `FS.Theme`, `FS.Config`, `FS.Gunsight`, `FS.GunsightBoxes`, `FS.GunsightTags`, `FS.GunsightAreas` (the Class module background slider under Target side reads its `SETTINGS.background`; left out without it), `FS.PriestRight` (the Priest class module group on the Gunsight HUD page reads its `SETTING`, `REMIND`, `Setting` and `Applies`; shown to a Priest only), `FS.FiveSecondTab` (the Mana regen group on the Unit Frames page reads its `SETTINGS`; the group is left out for a class without mana or a client without the tab), `FS.BuffTab` (the Shaman buff tab group on the Unit Frames page reads its `SETTINGS`; left out without the tab), `FS.MicroBars` and `FS.SpellArt` (the Icon style group on the Unit Frames page reads their `SETTING`, `STYLES` and `Style`; a row is left out without its module, the spell row also until a tab has drawn a spell icon)
- Also `FS.Nameplates` (the Nameplates group on the Unit Frames page reads its `SETTING`; left out without the module, which exports nothing on a client with no nameplate API), `FS.DamageMeterSkin` (the Damage meter group on the Unit Frames page reads its `SETTING`, `STYLES`, `STYLE_TEXT` and `Style`; left out without the skin), `FS.CharacterExport` (the Saved data group on the Profiles page reads its `SETTING` and `RELOAD_SETTING`; left out without the module), `FS.MinimapCoords` (the Minimap coordinates switch in the Panels group reads its `SETTING`; left out without the minimap module), `FS.PartyFrames` and `FS.RaidFrames` (the Party frames group on the Unit Frames page reads their `CONFIG_KEY`, and the raid switch its `NeedsReload` line; each row is left out without its module)
- Depended on by: nothing; modules expose settings, this file reads them
