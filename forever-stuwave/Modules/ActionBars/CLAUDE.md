# ActionBars

The Console, its bars and the class shoulders on its top edge. This file covers the shoulder tabs; the bars themselves are documented in their file headers.

## Key Files
- `ClassShoulder.lua` -- the shoulder chrome and the seats. `OWNERS` lists the class tabs (Paladin, Warrior, Shaman and the Shaman's `also` buff tab); `FIVE` is the five second rule tab, a shoulder of every mana class that is NOT an owner entry
- `BuffTab.lua` + `ShamanBuffs.lua` -- the Shaman IMBUE and SHIELD tab and its data. Each tile wears the ICON of the spell it casts (`C_Spell.GetSpellTexture` of the view's `spellId`: the imbue shown, Lightning Shield, Water Shield while that is up), the time or orb count as a small overlay on a label plate; missing and unreadable states dim and desaturate it; a spell the client gives no texture for keeps the mockup's chip label and is asked again after 2 s. Combat state (UNVERIFIED live): one `AuraContainer` on `player` inside the host holds a SHIELD `AddAuraSlot` (every Lightning and Water Shield rank id, rebuilt out of combat only when an id never seen before appears, at most 3 times a session) and main/off hand `AddItemEnchantment` slots (no spell filter: any weapon enchant lights the imbue tile in combat), each seated on the tile icon by its own `initializeFrame`; built, enabled and shown out of combat only, alpha 1 in combat and 0 out of it (the only combat write, slot regions are written out of combat only); in combat the tiles drop to a dim base with no frozen text, but only on proof (the container read back armed, every slot frame PROVEN on its tile's icon rect by a proof pass out of combat on every tick, read before anything re-seats it; a slot that cannot be read or was built in combat is unproven until the next such pass; any unproven tile also keeps the whole container at alpha 0; the mouse of each slot read back off) and with the `buffs.combatstate` setting on (default, a toggle on the Unit Frames page); otherwise the old frozen estimate. A failed build is one attempt per out of combat pass, three passes give it up, plus one uncounted retry 10 s after the failure from the tick; KNOWN LIMIT: a slot's position cannot be read in combat, so an engine re-anchor during a fight is not seen until the next out of combat pass; a hidden slot with no readable rect keeps a proof it already had, an unreadable slot is logged after 5 ticks, and a rebuild past the cap of 3 is logged ("/reload to cover it"); the live state line is `ForeverSTUwaveDB.buffTabEngine.line`, on disk after a /reload; a half-failed imbue pair parks the container and rebuilds with the shield slot only
- `Core/SpellArt.lua` + `Core/SpellArtData.lua` (generated by `tools/assets/synthwave_icons.py --bake-spells`, art in `Media/Textures/spellart_*.tga`) -- the gradient style B for the spell icons these tabs draw. Every icon write goes through `FS.FrameHelpers.SetSpellIcon(tex, stock)`, which maps the stock icon (file id or `Interface\Icons` path) to its baked twin, falls back to the stock icon for anything unmapped, and registers the texture so the `icons.spellstyle` setting (`gradient` default, `stock`; Unit Frames page) repaints it live. Texture writes are legal in combat. The engine-drawn `AuraContainer` slots of the buff tab (`part.lit`) are NOT routed through it and stay stock in combat. New .tga files need a full client restart, `/reload` does not load them
- `TotemPresets.lua` + `TotemDial.lua` -- the Shaman totem presets (mockups/totem-presets-2026-10-14.html, concept C): the DIAL, a seventh cell on the totem tab. See "Totem presets" below
- `FiveSecondTab.lua` -- the five second rule tab (mockups/five-second-rule-module-2026-10-14.html, concept C, CELLS)

## Five second rule tab (`FS.FiveSecondTab`)
- Two 38 px tiles on a second shoulder (92.8 x 49): CELLS (five cells that drain one a second, `~N.N` text, cyan REGEN at 0.0 with one rim pulse, a dim idle look a few seconds later) and RATE (mana per second plus a retention chip). Display only: plain frames `FSFiveSecondTab`, `FSFiveSecondCells`, `FSFiveSecondRate`, no secure button, no binding, no target gate.
- Who: Priest, Mage, Warlock, Hunter, Druid, Shaman, Paladin; never Warrior or Rogue. A Druid shows it in a mana form only (`UnitPowerType`, an unreadable answer shows it). The ledger keeps listening while it is hidden.
- Seat: `ClassShoulder.lua` works the x out on every Refresh, 16 design px right of the last class tab that stands (Paladin: after the 306.8 px seal block; Shaman: after the buff tab, at 424.8), 36 when the class has no tab. A moved seat tells the tab (`changed` -> `Sync`) like a shown or hidden one. No Console, no tab.
- Ledger (the whole truth is an ESTIMATE): `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED` for `"player"` with a plain type 0 cost above 0 in `C_Spell.GetSpellPowerCost` starts five seconds; a re-cast restarts them; a cost that cannot be read plainly starts nothing; mana is never read (`UnitPower` on mana is always secret). A mana channel holds the window at the full five seconds until `UNIT_SPELLCAST_CHANNEL_STOP` (or `UnitChannelInfo` saying nothing channels), then the five seconds run. Unverified on Forever: channels, cancelled casts, free casts from procs.
- Rate: `GetPowerRegen()` is plain out of combat only. It is read once a second and at regen, kept in combat, and the RATE underline is dashed while the numbers on show are the kept ones. Casting rate shows while the window runs, the full rate otherwise. Retention is `casting / full` as a percent (cyan at 1% or more, a hollow `0%` below).
- Settings (`FS.FiveSecondTab.SETTINGS`, read by `ConfigWindow.lua`'s Unit Frames page): `fivesecond.enabled` (default on), `fivesecond.pulse` (on), `fivesecond.sound` (off). Each is explained by its `tip`; the client text says only "five second rule" and "regen".
- Nothing here is protected, so building, painting, the master toggle and the Druid hide are legal in combat; the host's anchor onto the Console waits for `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED` inside `Sync`. A hidden Druid form or a master toggle flipped in combat leaves the shoulder art, an EMPTY tab outline with no tiles in it, until the next out of combat Refresh.
- Guesses to probe live: the sound (`SOUNDKIT.TELL_MESSAGE`), the channel default, `UnitPowerType` being plain for a Druid, and whether `GetPowerRegen` is secret in combat outside instances.
- `tools/fivesecond-harness.py` pins it (window, pulse, fade, rate cache, chip, tooltips, settings, seats for Priest, Paladin and Shaman, secrets, combat).

## Totem presets (`FS.TotemPresets`, `FS.TotemDial`)
- Data (`TotemPresets.lua`): up to 4 presets per character in `ForeverSTUwaveDB.totemPresets[guid] = { list = { { name, picks = { element = rank 1 spell id }, order = { elements } } }, active = n, gnarly = n|nil }`. `active` may be one past the list: the NEW slot (dial shows "+", selecting it changes nothing). Names are letters, digits and spaces, up to 8, uppercased. A saved file is sanitised when it loads. A read-only Config or a database that is not a table keeps the presets in memory for the session and refuses edits; the module never creates or replaces the database.
- Switching: every totem of the preset the character knows is picked through `TotemBar.Pick` (so the buttons, the native multi-cast set and the Gunsight poles follow), only on a button that does not already cast it. An element the preset leaves out keeps its pick. A totem not learned yet is LOCKED (dashed grey arc, "(level N)", skipped by the step). OUT OF COMBAT ONLY: a switch in combat is queued (`P.pending`, the dial shows it amber) and applied by `PS.OnRegen`, which `TotemBar.lua` calls at `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED` inside a pcall before its own catch-up. Editing (create, rename, fill, move, delete) and the editor card are refused in combat; the card closes when combat starts.
- Cells: `TotemBar.AddCell(def)` (before the tab builds) adds a button after the six: `FSTotemButton7` is the dial (key `dial`, cyan). `TotemBar.Cols()` is 6 plus the cells that are on; `ClassShoulder.lua` builds the shoulder per width (`colsFn`), and the buff tab and the five second rule tab follow with `after = 16`, so the tab is 264 px without the dial and 306.8 px with it and the others move 42.8 px right. A world without these two files stays the six stock buttons.
- Dial: click = the step (secure `type1="macro"`, `macrotext1="/castsequence reset=combat/6 A, B, C"`, a plain `/cast A` for one totem; written in `TotemDial.ApplyCell` from the totem tab's Sync, out of combat only, rewritten only when the text changes), `shift-type1`/`alt-type1` = "" so edit clicks never drop a totem. Right click and the mouse wheel cycle presets (each its own setting). Shift click edits the active preset; on the NEW slot it first creates a preset as a copy of the four buttons. A press is counted on the event the game acts on (`ActionButtonUseKeyDown`).
- Step record: our own, never read from the game. Advances on `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED` for the next totem by name; resets at combat end, after 6 s without a press or drop (`PS.Expire`, from the dial's ticker `FSTotemDialTicker`) and on any edit or switch. A stun or failed cast can leave the game's own sequence off by one from the record until it restarts.
- Look: a ring of one arc per totem (textures `Media/Textures/totem_dial_n{n}_{i}_{kind}.tga` from `tools/assets/generate_totem_dial.py`: t quiet, g done, k next, c core, d locked, plus `totem_dial_empty.tga`) tinted by element; name plate on the rail (`totems.dialPlate`); raised plate with ">NAME" while pending or "k/n" while stepping; pending = amber dashed border, triangle, underline and amber dashed marks on the buttons that will change; applied = cyan marks for 1.6 s. The look is texture writes on plain regions only, so it is legal in combat.
- Editor: a plain card on UIParent (`FSTotemEditor`, DIALOG, closes on Escape), rows for the drop order (totem tile opens the chooser of known totems plus "left out", up and down arrows), name box (commits on Enter or focus loss), two step DELETE. Nothing secure is anchored to it.
- Settings (each with a ? tip and its own off switch, Unit Frames page group "Totem presets"): `totems.dial`, `totems.dialPlate`, `totems.dialRight`, `totems.dialWheel`, `totems.waitPulse`, `totems.waitSound` (off), `totems.appliedFlash`, `totems.appliedSound` (off).
- State line: `ForeverSTUwaveDB.totemPresetState.line` (on disk after a /reload) reports presets, active, pending, the dial's `type1`, script length, step k/n, presses and matched casts, and `castids=secret` when a cast's spell id was a secret value. Never created over a missing or read-only database.
- UNVERIFIED in game (built defensively, never claimed): that a secure `/castsequence reset=combat/6` run from the dial casts one totem per press on Forever and restarts as written; that `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED`'s spell id is plain for the player's totems; the `SOUNDKIT` names of the two cues (a client without them plays nothing); the cell's fonts and arcs at the live scales. Needs a FULL client restart after install (new files in the toc and a new binding), not a /reload.
- `tools/totempresets-harness.py` pins it (data, caps, sanitising, switching and the combat queue, the step script and record, the face, tooltips, the editor, the seats, the state line, secrets and hygiene); `tools/assets/generate_totem_dial.py` makes the arcs.
