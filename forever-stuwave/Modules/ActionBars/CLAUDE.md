# ActionBars

The Console, its bars and the class shoulders on its top edge. This file covers the shoulder tabs; the bars themselves are documented in their file headers.

## Key Files
- `ClassShoulder.lua` -- the shoulder chrome and the seats. `OWNERS` lists the class tabs (Paladin, Warrior, Shaman and the Shaman's `also` buff tab); `FIVE` is the mana regen module, a shoulder of every mana class that is NOT an owner entry
- `BuffTab.lua` + `ShamanBuffs.lua` -- the Shaman IMBUE and SHIELD tab and its data. Each tile wears the ICON of the spell it casts (`C_Spell.GetSpellTexture` of the view's `spellId`: the imbue shown, Lightning Shield, Water Shield while that is up), the time or orb count as a small overlay on a label plate (up states only); missing and unreadable states dim and desaturate it, and a MISSING tile (`warn`, or the engine mode in combat; not the hollow `?`) shows its word IMBUE or SHIELD on a BAND instead: `fsBand`, an opaque label plate colour texture (alpha .96, 9 design px tall from row 24, 32 wide, 3 in from each side, below the ring and above the underline) and `fsWord`, a 9 px red fontstring of its own centred on it, both on the tile's face frame so the engine cover hides them when the buff is up; the time line and its plate are off while the band is on, and the band and word are off in every up state (`SeatBand` seats both once per scale out of combat on whole physical pixels (pixels per UI unit = `FrameHelpers.PixelsPerUnit`, i.e. PixelUtil's factor over the effective scale, NOT the effective scale: the player's 0.64 with physical height 1440 is 1.2 pixels per unit), the band an even number of pixels wide, the word has NO box of its own (a fontstring with a width is cut to "SH..." by the client when the text is wider; one without a width just grows, so at the smallest size the word overflows the band and is never cut), only a centre on the middle of a box one pixel below the band's top and an even height, so the centred word stays off half pixels and its capitals' ink (Mononoki Bold: cap .703, ascent .879, descent .244 em) stays inside the band; an odd pixel tile leaves the margins one pixel apart; the word is 9 PHYSICAL pixels (set in UI units as 9 / pixels per unit; `WORD_PX` is not scaled by the layout scale), a model start (9, or less when the band is narrow or short), then SHRUNK in half pixels until the width the client MEASURES (`GetUnboundedStringWidth`/`GetStringWidth`, hinted advances run wider than the font file's) fits the band less a pixel, never in a paint; a paint only shows, hides, colours and sets the text, so combat writes no point, size or font); a spell the client gives no texture for keeps the mockup's chip label and is asked again after 2 s. Combat state (UNVERIFIED live): one `AuraContainer` on `player` inside the host holds a SHIELD `AddAuraSlot` (every Lightning and Water Shield rank id, rebuilt out of combat only when an id never seen before appears, at most 3 times a session) and main/off hand `AddItemEnchantment` slots (no spell filter: any weapon enchant lights the imbue tile in combat), each seated on the tile icon by its own `initializeFrame`; built, enabled and shown out of combat only, alpha 1 in combat and 0 out of it (the only combat write, slot regions are written out of combat only); in combat the tile wears EXACTLY its out of combat missing look (`looks.engine` = `looks.warn` with the glow off: red ring, red mixed plate, dim desaturated icon, red IMBUE or SHIELD word on its band and underline; only the red glow is left out because nothing can cover it) and no frozen text; the slot frame carries the lit look as children (`part.coverPlate` opaque tile-sized plate and `part.coverRing` opaque ring, both seated once out of combat by `CoverSlot`, anchored to the TILE), and the engine hides the slot frame when its aura is absent, so the cover vanishes with the buff and the red shows through, lit again on recast (no slot state is read in combat; the out of combat passes read IsShown, size and lit texture). The IMBUE tile goes live in combat only after a pass out of combat SAW a slot shown while the plain read said an imbue is on (`fsEngine.seen`); until then, or if this client never draws item enchantment slots, it keeps the old counting down estimate. The tile's key label (and its plate) rides `fsHotkeyHost`, a frame above the container (at least host level + `ENGINE_LEVEL` + 3 and one over the highest level found under the container, its slots and their child frames, since a created child sits at its parent's level + 1; re-levelled out of combat only, by the host seat and the engine passes, so a frame the engine adds later is covered at the next pass), so the cover never hides it; the cover plate's slice margin is `Theme.SLICE_CUT_MARGIN` like the tile plate's. The state line reports each slot's IsShown, size, lit texture and cover (`slotN shown=.. WxH lit=.. cover=plate+ring|plate|ring|none`, the cover is built under a pcall so `none` means this client refused it; read out of combat only). The engine path runs only on proof (the container read back armed, every slot frame PROVEN on its tile's icon rect by a proof pass out of combat on every tick, read before anything re-seats it; a slot that cannot be read or was built in combat is unproven until the next such pass; any unproven tile also keeps the whole container at alpha 0; the mouse of each slot read back off) and with the `buffs.combatstate` setting on (default, a toggle on the Unit Frames page); otherwise the old frozen estimate. A failed build is one attempt per out of combat pass, three passes give it up, plus one uncounted retry 10 s after the failure from the tick; KNOWN LIMIT: a slot's position cannot be read in combat, so an engine re-anchor during a fight is not seen until the next out of combat pass; an idle slot (IsShown false, or width and height both plain 0, since the client keeps IsShown secret: the off hand slot of a one handed Shaman reads 0x0 with no rect) with no readable rect keeps a proof it already had and is never logged (a slot never proven stays unproven, so the container stays hidden), a non idle slot whose rect cannot be read is logged after 5 ticks, and a rebuild past the cap of 3 is logged ("/reload to cover it"); the live state line is `ForeverSTUwaveDB.buffTabEngine.line`, on disk after a /reload (every slot value in it is read out of combat by `ReadPart` and written through `Num`/`Plain`/`CodeText`, so a secret reads `secret` or `?` and never throws; a failed write goes to the quiet degrade log, `FS.LogQuiet`, with its error text, never to chat; a slot whose `IsShown` is not a plain boolean falls back to `IsVisible`, else reads `shown=?(why)` with why = secret, error, type:<t> or missing for each call (live 6eeab06: every slot read `shown=?`, so the `seen` gate never opened, probably because a slot that draws an aura is secret by design); the shield aura read treats a secret aura, name, id or list as unknown, never compared); a half-failed imbue pair parks the container and rebuilds with the shield slot only
- `Core/SpellArt.lua` over `Core/IconStyle.lua` and `Core/IconStyleData.lua` (the data is generated by `tools/assets/synthwave_icons.py --gen-runtime`: numbers only, no art) -- the runtime gradient style for the spell icons these tabs draw. The tile keeps drawing the game's OWN texture; `IconStyle` stacks three overlay textures over it (an ADD copy for brightness, one of our `iconramp_<family>.tga` in blend mode MOD, a flat ADD lift) and the base is forced desaturated, so no art derived from the game is shipped. Every icon write goes through `FS.FrameHelpers.SetSpellIcon(tex, stock)`, which looks the stock icon (file id or `Interface\Icons` path) up in the data (`FS.SpellArt.Look`), falls back to the plain stock icon for anything unmapped, and registers the texture so the `icons.spellstyle` setting (`gradient` default, `stock`; Unit Frames page) repaints it live. The tiles keep tinting, dimming, desaturating, showing, hiding and texcoord-cropping their icon texture the way they always did: post-hooks on those setters (`hooksecurefunc` on the texture) copy the state onto the overlays, so a new tile site needs nothing but `SetSpellIcon` and free draw sublevels (3) above its icon in the same layer (a texture at sublevel 5 or more is lowered so the chain fits under the engine's 7). The chain is never BUILT in combat (it creates, layers and anchors regions of a tile that may sit on a secure host): a tile that asks for a look in combat keeps the stock icon and is dressed at `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED`; an existing chain only gets texture, colour and alpha writes. A dimmed tile (alpha under 1) or a tinted one (a channel under 0.98: the stance bar's 0.4, the buff tab's warn red) loses its MOD ramp (it ignores tint and alpha, a red times a cyan ramp is black) and keeps the ADD copy and lift, which carry the tint and alpha; alpha 0 hides every overlay. Icon values with no look are listed once per session in `ForeverSTUwaveDB.buffTabEngine.unmappedIcons` (on disk after a /reload) to show which spells the client reports with ids the table lacks. The engine-drawn `AuraContainer` slots of the buff tab (`part.lit`) are NOT routed through it and stay stock in combat. New .tga files (ramps, covers) need a full client restart, `/reload` does not load them. The micro keys (`MicroBars.lua`) use the same chain for the game's micro button textures and the player's class icon (`GlyphSpec`: Character reads `UnitClass`'s token when the key is built, guarded with `FS.IsSecret` and type, and falls back to our own baked person glyph); the icon is a rectangle over the key's inside (columns 1 to 33 of 34, the cover's columns, full height) and our `iconcover_key.tga` (the plate colour where the shape is not, cut to the ring's inner edge so no band of plate colour is left between the ring and the art) trims the two outside triangles along the slant, because masks do not clip on this client. The key leans `/`, so a narrower rectangle would leave two wedges of the plate without art; the rectangle spans the whole inside and every glyph is UPRIGHT (no lean, no shear) and zoomed to fill it: its four texcoords are a window of the texture with the rectangle's aspect (`fit_window` in `synthwave_icons.py`; the data's `size` is the texture's texel size, the harness checks the aspect to 2%), cropped at the edge of the glyph's own part of the texture where that is too narrow or too short (a classic button face is 22 x 29 texels, so it loses about a third of its height), never stretched. When the layout shrinks the keys (more keys than the slot holds) the rectangle gets narrower at the same height, so `SizeKey` refits the window to the rendered rectangle's aspect (`FitWindow` in `MicroBars.lua`: same height and centre, inside the data's `limit`, the glyph's own part of the texture, shrinking the height if the width cannot fit; `SetWindow` writes it to the glyph and its ADD copy; the class icon too). The twelve classic button micro keys carry a `zoom` in the data (`MICRO_ZOOM`, one shared value, 0.5, the approved moderate level; and `MICRO_ZOOM_OVERRIDE` per key in `synthwave_icons.py`; a classic button face cropped to the middle reads as a blob): `GlyphView` grows the window's height from the close-up crop (0) to the glyph's whole `limit` (1) at the same width and centre, and `SizeKey` then draws it in a narrower rectangle of the window's own aspect, centred in the inside (the plate shows at the two sides), still on whole pixels; the cover and the MOD ramp and lift keep the whole inside. The atlas keys, the faction emblems and the class icons have no zoom and keep the v5 window (the harness pins the data text). Transparent parts of an atlas icon or an emblem still show the plate. Only the glyph and its ADD copy carry coords; the MOD ramp and the lift span the cover's rectangle. Pieces sit on whole physical pixels (`PixelUnit`, like the mana regen module's `FT.Snap`) (top and bottom edges too) with the engine's pixel snapping on and texel bias 0, and a disabled micro key drops its MOD ramp like a dimmed spell tile. If `UnitClass` or the faction was unknown at build, `PLAYER_ENTERING_WORLD` (out of combat) dresses the key again. The micro keys have their own setting, `icons.microstyle` (`stock` default, `gradient`; the "Icon style" group on the Unit Frames page lists it beside `icons.spellstyle`, each with a ? tip): stock draws the game's own texture untinted and not desaturated (a disabled key still dims and greys, that is state), still cut by the cover, and releases the chain; it repaints every key live (texture writes only, legal in combat); our own baked keys look the same in both. Only `glyphart_character.tga` and `glyphart_legacy.tga` are baked art (hand drawn shapes of ours); `tools/assets/test_tga_generators.py` fails if any other shipped texture has no generator.
- `TotemPresets.lua` + `TotemDial.lua` -- the Shaman totem presets (the design mockup): two preset cells, Preset 1 (cell 7) and Preset 2 (cell 8), on the totem tab, each with its own outline colour. See "Totem presets" below
- `FiveSecondTab.lua` -- the mana regen module (the design mockup, face D2 with digit option B; art `Media/Textures/led_segments.tga`, `led_segment_glow.tga`, `led_glow.tga` + `led_well.tga` from `tools/assets/generate_led_segments.py`; harness `tools/fivesecond-harness.py`)

## Mana regen module (`FS.FiveSecondTab`)
- The code names keep `FiveSecondTab` and the `fivesecond.*` setting keys (saved settings must not break); everything a player reads says "Mana regen" and never "five second rule" or "5 sec" (a harness check scans the strings).
- One face on a second shoulder (92.8 x 49, LED style): a hero tile (gauge of ten segments over a 7 cell readout, two digits plus the five letter REGEN word, 14 segments per cell cut from `led_segments.tga`, one texture per segment at native pixel size, cyan REGEN at 0.0 with one rim pulse, a dim idle look a few seconds later) and the rate line (mana per second as text over a dashed bar, plus a retention chip).
- Zones: `FSFiveSecondCells` (hero tile and gauge, tooltip "cells") and `FSFiveSecondRate` (rate line and chip, tooltip "rate") over the host `FSFiveSecondTab`. Display only: plain frames, no secure button, no binding, no target gate.
- Pixels: sizes and offsets are placed edge by edge on whole physical pixels (`SeatGrid`, `FT.Place`, `FT.Snap`; texels per cell pixel `n` comes from the screen height, so under 1440 px high the readout sits flush with the ring). `Crisp()` sets SetSnapToPixelGrid and SetTexelSnappingBias; the glow is its own ADD texture, a soft copy under each lit piece and never a block behind the readout (`led_segment_glow.tga` is the segment atlas blurred and sits under each lit segment, tilde bar and dot; `led_glow.tga` is a soft box stretched over each gauge segment plus one design px each side, in its colour, none at rest, so no halo leaves the gauge well or the tile). The hero plate is `led_well.tga`, the page's violet gradient baked into a two corner chamfer nine-slice and drawn white (never tinted; the idle dimming is the zone's alpha), with `FT.shine`, the page's 1 px white 0.08 line two px under the top edge. The outer frame is the shared `ClassShoulder` chassis, not this tab's. Never a blur on a segment. New TGAs need a full client restart.
- Motion: a pure function of the ledger and the clock (`Motion(now)`; `Tab.Fx()` exposes the values), no AnimationGroups except the idle glow, which is not on the ticker: it is looping Alpha AnimationGroups (rim ring and glow, plus one per gauge segment; `FT.breath`, `SetBreath`), stopped by the count, a switch or a hidden tab (under the mouse only the rim stops, the gauge keeps breathing). A per frame ticker runs only while an effect is live (`FT.fast`), a 0.05 s one (`FT.ambient`) in the regen hold and just around the kept rate stutter, 0.5 s otherwise; the ticker frame `FSFiveSecondTicker` is a child of the tab, so a hidden tab or a hidden interface (Alt+Z) has no OnUpdate at all and the idle glow costs nothing while the interface is hidden (nothing repaints; its groups play unseen and the glow is right again on the first tick after the interface returns), and the motion model gates on `IsVisible()`. Alpha animation values are in the region's own alpha space (cited in the `BreathGroup` comment); a missing rim or gauge piece logs once to the quiet degrade log. With the master off no pulse is started or counted and nothing holds the per frame ticker but the count itself. Paints are gated by a signature (`FT.sigCells`, `FT.sigRate`), so a still tab writes nothing. At most one strong motion at a time.
- Who: Priest, Mage, Warlock, Hunter, Druid, Shaman, Paladin; never Warrior or Rogue. A Druid shows it in a mana form only (`UnitPowerType`, an unreadable answer shows it). The ledger keeps listening while it is hidden.
- Seat: `ClassShoulder.lua` works the x out on every Refresh, 16 design px right of the last class tab that stands (Paladin: after the 306.8 px seal block; Shaman: after the buff tab, at 424.8), 36 when the class has no tab. A moved seat tells the tab (`changed` -> `Sync`) like a shown or hidden one. No Console, no tab.
- Ledger (the whole truth is an ESTIMATE): `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED` for `"player"` with a plain type 0 cost above 0 in `C_Spell.GetSpellPowerCost` starts five seconds; a re-cast restarts them; a cost that cannot be read plainly starts nothing; mana is never read (`UnitPower` on mana is always secret). A mana channel holds the window at the full five seconds until `UNIT_SPELLCAST_CHANNEL_STOP` (or `UnitChannelInfo` saying nothing channels), then the five seconds run. Unverified on Forever: channels, cancelled casts, free casts from procs.
- Rate: `GetPowerRegen()` is plain out of combat only. It is read once a second and at regen, kept in combat, and the RATE underline is dashed while the numbers on show are the kept ones. Casting rate shows while the window runs, the full rate otherwise. Retention is `casting / full` as a percent (cyan at 1% or more, a hollow `0%` below).
- Settings (`FS.FiveSecondTab.SETTINGS`, read by `ConfigWindow.lua`'s Unit Frames page): `fivesecond.enabled` (default on), `fivesecond.pulse` (on, also gates the rim flare), `fivesecond.sound` (off), `fivesecond.motion` (on, the master of all motion) and one switch for each effect in `FT.FX_NAMES` (`fivesecond.fx.flash`, `digits`, `wpulse`, `drop`, `sweep`, `tick`, `breath`, `glitch`, all on; the page lists them indented under the master). Each is explained by its `tip`; the client text says only "mana regen" and "regen".
- Nothing here is protected, so building, painting, the master toggle and the Druid hide are legal in combat; the host's anchor onto the Console waits for `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED` inside `Sync`. A hidden Druid form or a master toggle flipped in combat leaves the shoulder art, an EMPTY tab outline with no tiles in it, until the next out of combat Refresh.
- Guesses to probe live: the sound (`SOUNDKIT.TELL_MESSAGE`), the channel default, `UnitPowerType` being plain for a Druid, and whether `GetPowerRegen` is secret in combat outside instances.
- `tools/fivesecond-harness.py` pins it (window, pulse, fade, rate cache, chip, tooltips, settings, seats for Priest, Paladin and Shaman, secrets, combat).

## Totem presets (`FS.TotemPresets`, `FS.TotemDial`)
- Data (`TotemPresets.lua`): exactly two slots per character in `ForeverSTUwaveDB.totemPresets[guid] = { slots = { [1] = preset|nil, [2] = preset|nil }, colors = { n1, n2 }, retired = { preset... } }`, a preset being `{ name, picks = { element = rank 1 spell id }, order = { elements } }`. A slot with no preset is UNSET. Names are letters, digits and spaces, up to 8, uppercased; the first totem put on an unset slot (or `PS.Create` from the four buttons) makes a preset named `PRESET n`. The colour belongs to the cell, not the preset: an index into `P.COLORS` (cyan 22e0ff, pink ff2e97, violet a855f7, amber ffb648, green 39ff14, red ff3b4e, white f3fbff), defaults 1 and 2, kept by CLEAR, and a bad index reads as the slot's default. MIGRATION runs once when a character's store is first read (a file with no `slots`): the old list/active/GNARLY shape becomes Preset 1 = the active preset (the first when the active index was past the list), Preset 2 = the GNARLY preset when it differs, else the first other preset in list order, else unset; any further presets go to `retired`, never deleted; running it again changes nothing. A saved file is sanitised when it loads. A read-only Config, a database that is not a table, or a character GUID that cannot be read yet (never a made up key) keeps the presets in memory for the session and refuses edits; the module never creates or replaces the database.
- Nothing is switched: there is no dial, no active preset, no queue and no list. Every totem of a preset the character knows is dropped by that cell's own script; a totem not learned yet is LOCKED (dashed grey arc, "(level N)", skipped by the step). Editing (fill, move, rename, colour, CLEAR) and the editor card are refused in combat and the card closes when combat starts; a press never waits for anything.
- Cells: `TotemBar.AddCell(def)` (before the tab builds) adds a button after the six: `FSTotemButton7` is Preset 1 (key `preset1`, setting `totems.dial`, default cyan), `FSTotemButton8` is Preset 2 (key `preset2`, setting `totems.gnarly`, default pink); the setting keys keep their old names so saved choices carry over. `TotemBar.Cols()` is 6 plus the cells that are on (a cell that is off hides and the next steps into its column); `ClassShoulder.lua` builds the shoulder per width (`colsFn`), and the buff tab and the mana regen module follow with `after = 16`: the tab is 264 px with no cell, 306.8 with one, 349.6 with both, and the others move right with it. A world without these two files stays the six stock buttons.
- Click: the step (secure `type1="macro"`, `macrotext1="/castsequence reset=combat/6 A, B, C"`, a plain `/cast A` for one totem; written in `TotemDial.ApplyCell` from the totem tab's Sync, out of combat only, rewritten only when the text changes, and blanked for an unset slot), `shift-type1`, `alt-type1`, `alt-shift-type1`, `alt-ctrl-type1`, `alt-ctrl-shift-type1` and `ctrl-shift-type1` = "" (`EDIT_CLICKS`; a combo with no attribute of its own falls back to `type1`) so edit clicks never drop a totem, and a keybind that includes Shift edits instead of dropping (the config tip says so). The preset code reads the known totems through `PS.Known` (a per element cache of `TotemBar.KnownList`, dropped by `Dial.Refresh`, the learn events and regen). Shift click edits (`Dial.EditSlot`): on a set cell it opens its preset, on an unset one it makes the preset from the four totem buttons first (nothing is made when they give no totem) and opens the editor either way; refused in combat. An unset cell does nothing when pressed and shows no disabled look. A press is counted on the event the game acts on (`ActionButtonUseKeyDown`).
- Outline colour: per slot, one of seven swatches in the editor, saved as `colors`. It paints the cell's border, glow, underline and name label with vertex colours on plain textures and fontstrings, so painting is legal in combat (only choosing it is out of combat); the arcs keep their element colours. Hover tints the cell fill 16% toward the colour and takes the glow to .6, pressed 32% (`OnEnter/OnLeave/OnMouseDown/OnMouseUp/OnHide` hooks on the secure cell write textures only). An UNSET cell is dim (a face-coloured tint), has a muted dashed border (ring and glow off), a muted "+" in the middle, a muted underline at .5 and the plate UNSET.
- Step record: our own, never read from the game. Advances on `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED` for the next totem by name, for the cell whose press came within 2 s before the cast (the game advances a sequence only when its own button runs it; a cast with no press counts for nobody, and when two cells match the later press owns it); resets at combat end, after 6 s without a press or drop (`PS.Expire`, from the dial's ticker `FSTotemDialTicker`) and on any edit. A stun or failed cast can leave the game's own sequence off by one from the record until it restarts.
- Look: a ring of one arc per totem (textures `Media/Textures/totem_dial_n{n}_{i}_{kind}.tga` from `tools/assets/generate_totem_dial.py`: t quiet, g done, k next, c core, d locked, plus `totem_dial_empty.tga`) tinted by element; the slot number in the middle (the next totem's icon while stepping, with cyan corner brackets); name plate on the rail above the cell in the outline colour (`totems.dialPlate`); a raised plate with "k/n" while stepping. The look is texture writes on plain regions only, so it is legal in combat.
- Editor: a plain card on UIParent (`FSTotemEditor`, DIALOG, 198 x 232 design px, closes on Escape; one card for both slots, anchored above the last shown cell). From the top: title, name box (commits on Enter or focus loss; a name typed on an unset slot makes an empty preset that holds it), OUTLINE and a row of seven 20 px swatches on a 27 px pitch (quiet; hover and pressed tint the fill and bring the ring to full alpha; the selected one wears a white outer ring, an inner ring in its colour and a bigger dot; click chooses the slot's colour), DROP ORDER and four rows, then CLEAR. Each row is ONE button across its whole width (`BuildRow`: bar, number, icon box, name; hover tints the fill 16% toward the violet ring and brings the ring to full alpha, pressed 32%, the icon box ring rises from .35 to .9; a left out row has a dashed muted box, 2 pixels on and 2 off, with a size 9 "--" and no ring) and opens the chooser of known totems plus "left out"; its two arrows are small buttons on top that light the row edge. CLEAR is one full width button that empties the totems and the name but keeps the colour: it asks twice (the second label is SURE?, armed for `D.CONFIRM` seconds, the card stays open on the empty slot), is disabled when the slot is empty and is available for Preset 1 too. The close X and the row arrows are drawn as one pixel squares (`SeatGlyph`, snapped to the pixel grid, margins equal on both axes) in even boxes. Every card offset, size and edge is rounded to whole physical pixels (`Es(frame)` = `FrameHelpers.PixelsPerUnit`: the pixel factor over the effective scale; the effective scale alone is not the pixel count); the card width is an even number of pixels so margins and full width controls are symmetric. Every editor control has hover, pressed and disabled looks (config window convention: fill mixed 0.16 toward the ring on hover, 0.32 pressed, ring to full alpha, label white; disabled is the piece at alpha 0.4 and takes no clicks (`SetEnabled` false on every button, row, swatch, chooser tile and the name box) while `PS.Refusal()` is set; opening the chooser, a swatch click and a chooser tile click are refused with the reason shown through `UIErrorsFrame`, and the ticker re-runs `RefreshCard` on an open card the moment the refusal begins or clears, so "editable in a moment" unlocks it). Hover writes only textures and colours. Nothing secure is anchored to the card.
- Set 2: Preset 2's totems are also written into the native multi-cast set 2 (Call of the Ancestors, level 30) by `PS.SyncSet2`, run from `Dial.Refresh` (so after every totem tab Sync). Out of combat only; only after every wanted element's set 1 slot is proven to map (the native `MultiCastActionButton` answers the formula id, or with none the slot holds that element's totem); set 1 is read before and after and must read the same (a changed slot is restored and set 2 is dead for the session, `set1Intact=false`), and until the sends are judged a set 1 slot that changed to the spell sent to its set 2 twin counts as the same bleed (the tab adopts its slot as the pick, so a comparison with the pick would see nothing; `set1Check=bracket|window|window+bracket|none` in the state line says which ran; any `TotemBar.Pick` (a preset switch or the picker, counted by `MC.PickSerial`) closes the window, and a set 1 slot that changed to a totem Preset 2 wants is counted unproven, never put back); a slot that does not read the wanted spell is sent it again after `TB.SETTLE` seconds, at most `P.SET2_ATTEMPTS` times per slot and spell (a spell that comes back later is a new attempt), and a send that did not show schedules a re-look as the picks do (`MC.ScheduleVerify`); a set 2 slot is written only when it reads back and holds nothing or a totem of its element, else it is left alone and counted `unproven=N`; unreadable set 1 slots or no Call of the Ancestors write nothing (state `wait`). Turning the setting off leaves set 2 as it is. No chat line. `totems.gnarlySet2` turns it off. Element helpers come from `TotemBar.MC` (the tab's own SendPick/ReadSlot/ActionId/NativeActionId). Set 3 (Call of the Spirits, level 40) is treated as absent.
- Settings (each with a ? tip, Unit Frames page group "Totem presets"): `totems.dial` (Preset 1), `totems.gnarly` (Preset 2), `totems.dialPlate` (name plates) and `totems.gnarlySet2` (Preset 2 also on Call of the Ancestors).
