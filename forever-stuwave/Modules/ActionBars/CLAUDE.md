# ActionBars

The Console, its bars and the class shoulders on its top edge. This file covers the shoulder tabs; the bars themselves are documented in their file headers.

## Key Files
- `ClassShoulder.lua` -- the shoulder chrome and the seats. `OWNERS` lists the class tabs (Paladin, Warrior, Shaman and the Shaman's `also` buff tab); `FIVE` is the five second rule tab, a shoulder of every mana class that is NOT an owner entry
- `BuffTab.lua` + `ShamanBuffs.lua` -- the Shaman IMBUE and SHIELD tab and its data. Each tile wears the ICON of the spell it casts (`C_Spell.GetSpellTexture` of the view's `spellId`: the imbue shown, Lightning Shield, Water Shield while that is up), the time or orb count as a small overlay on a label plate; missing and unreadable states dim and desaturate it; a spell the client gives no texture for keeps the mockup's chip label and is asked again after 2 s. Combat state (UNVERIFIED live): one `AuraContainer` on `player` inside the host holds a SHIELD `AddAuraSlot` (every Lightning and Water Shield rank id, rebuilt out of combat only when an id never seen before appears, at most 3 times a session) and main/off hand `AddItemEnchantment` slots (no spell filter: any weapon enchant lights the imbue tile in combat), each seated on the tile icon by its own `initializeFrame`; built, enabled and shown out of combat only, alpha 1 in combat and 0 out of it (the only combat write, slot regions are written out of combat only); in combat the tile wears EXACTLY its out of combat missing look (`looks.engine` = `looks.warn` with the glow off: red ring, red mixed plate, dim desaturated icon, red IMBUE or SHIELD word and underline at the normal layout; only the red glow is left out because nothing can cover it) and no frozen text; the slot frame carries the lit look as children (`part.coverPlate` opaque tile-sized plate and `part.coverRing` opaque ring, both seated once out of combat by `CoverSlot`, anchored to the TILE), and the engine hides the slot frame when its aura is absent, so the cover vanishes with the buff and the red shows through, lit again on recast (no slot state is read in combat; the out of combat passes read IsShown, size and lit texture). The IMBUE tile goes live in combat only after a pass out of combat SAW a slot shown while the plain read said an imbue is on (`fsEngine.seen`); until then, or if this client never draws item enchantment slots, it keeps the old counting down estimate. The tile's key label (and its plate) rides `fsHotkeyHost`, a frame above the container (host level + `ENGINE_LEVEL` + 1, seated out of combat), so the cover never hides it; the cover plate's slice margin is `Theme.SLICE_CUT_MARGIN` like the tile plate's. The state line reports each slot's IsShown, size, lit texture and cover (`slotN shown=.. WxH lit=.. cover=plate+ring|plate|ring|none`, the cover is built under a pcall so `none` means this client refused it; read out of combat only). The engine path runs only on proof (the container read back armed, every slot frame PROVEN on its tile's icon rect by a proof pass out of combat on every tick, read before anything re-seats it; a slot that cannot be read or was built in combat is unproven until the next such pass; any unproven tile also keeps the whole container at alpha 0; the mouse of each slot read back off) and with the `buffs.combatstate` setting on (default, a toggle on the Unit Frames page); otherwise the old frozen estimate. A failed build is one attempt per out of combat pass, three passes give it up, plus one uncounted retry 10 s after the failure from the tick; KNOWN LIMIT: a slot's position cannot be read in combat, so an engine re-anchor during a fight is not seen until the next out of combat pass; a hidden slot with no readable rect keeps a proof it already had, an unreadable slot is logged after 5 ticks, and a rebuild past the cap of 3 is logged ("/reload to cover it"); the live state line is `ForeverSTUwaveDB.buffTabEngine.line`, on disk after a /reload; a half-failed imbue pair parks the container and rebuilds with the shield slot only
- `Core/SpellArt.lua` over `Core/IconStyle.lua` and `Core/IconStyleData.lua` (the data is generated by `tools/assets/synthwave_icons.py --gen-runtime`: numbers only, no art) -- the runtime gradient style for the spell icons these tabs draw. The tile keeps drawing the game's OWN texture; `IconStyle` stacks three overlay textures over it (an ADD copy for brightness, one of our `iconramp_<family>.tga` in blend mode MOD, a flat ADD lift) and the base is forced desaturated, so no art derived from the game is shipped. Every icon write goes through `FS.FrameHelpers.SetSpellIcon(tex, stock)`, which looks the stock icon (file id or `Interface\Icons` path) up in the data (`FS.SpellArt.Look`), falls back to the plain stock icon for anything unmapped, and registers the texture so the `icons.spellstyle` setting (`gradient` default, `stock`; Unit Frames page) repaints it live. The tiles keep tinting, dimming, desaturating, showing, hiding and texcoord-cropping their icon texture the way they always did: post-hooks on those setters (`hooksecurefunc` on the texture) copy the state onto the overlays, so a new tile site needs nothing but `SetSpellIcon` and free draw sublevels (3) above its icon in the same layer (a texture at sublevel 5 or more is lowered so the chain fits under the engine's 7). The chain is never BUILT in combat (it creates, layers and anchors regions of a tile that may sit on a secure host): a tile that asks for a look in combat keeps the stock icon and is dressed at `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED`; an existing chain only gets texture, colour and alpha writes. A dimmed tile (alpha under 1) or a tinted one (a channel under 0.98: the stance bar's 0.4, the buff tab's warn red) loses its MOD ramp (it ignores tint and alpha, a red times a cyan ramp is black) and keeps the ADD copy and lift, which carry the tint and alpha; alpha 0 hides every overlay. Icon values with no look are listed once per session in `ForeverSTUwaveDB.buffTabEngine.unmappedIcons` (on disk after a /reload) to show which spells the client reports with ids the table lacks. The engine-drawn `AuraContainer` slots of the buff tab (`part.lit`) are NOT routed through it and stay stock in combat. New .tga files (ramps, covers) need a full client restart, `/reload` does not load them. The micro keys (`MicroBars.lua`) use the same chain for the game's micro button textures and the player's class icon (`GlyphSpec`: Character reads `UnitClass`'s token when the key is built, guarded with `FS.IsSecret` and type, and falls back to our own baked person glyph); the icon is a rectangle over the key's inside (columns 2 to 32 of 34, `data.rects.k`, so no plate wedge is left empty on the `/` leaning key) and our `iconcover_key.tga` (the plate colour where the shape is not) trims the two outside triangles along the slant, because masks do not clip on this client. A glyph the client's texture cannot be widened to that rectangle (a micro button's face with its frame, a round emblem) is drawn narrower (rects a, b) over a FILL, a flat 3x3 patch of the same texture stretched over the whole inside, with its own ADD copy under the glyph; the MOD ramp and the lift span the whole inside. Pieces sit on whole physical pixels (`PixelUnit`, like the five second tab's `FT.Snap`) with the engine's snapping off and texel bias 0. If `UnitClass` or the faction was unknown at build, `PLAYER_ENTERING_WORLD` (out of combat) dresses the key again. The micro keys have their own setting, `icons.microstyle` (`gradient` default, `stock`; the "Icon style" group on the Unit Frames page lists it beside `icons.spellstyle`, each with a ? tip): stock draws the game's own texture untinted and not desaturated (a disabled key still dims and greys, that is state), still cut by the cover, and releases the chain; it repaints every key live (texture writes only, legal in combat); our own baked keys look the same in both. Only `glyphart_character.tga` and `glyphart_legacy.tga` are baked art (hand drawn shapes of ours); `tools/assets/test_tga_generators.py` fails if any other shipped texture has no generator.
- `TotemPresets.lua` + `TotemDial.lua` -- the Shaman totem presets (mockups/totem-presets-2026-10-14.html, concept C): the DIAL, the dial (cell 7) and the GNARLY step cell (cell 8) on the totem tab. See "Totem presets" below
- `FiveSecondTab.lua` -- the five second rule tab (mockups/five-second-rule-compact-2026-10-14.html, face D2 with digit option B; art `Media/Textures/led_segments.tga` + `led_glow.tga` from `tools/assets/generate_led_segments.py`; harness `tools/fivesecond-harness.py`)

## Five second rule tab (`FS.FiveSecondTab`)
- One face on a second shoulder (92.8 x 49, LED style): a hero tile (gauge of ten segments over a 7 cell readout, two digits plus the five letter REGEN word, 14 segments per cell cut from `led_segments.tga`, one texture per segment at native pixel size, cyan REGEN at 0.0 with one rim pulse, a dim idle look a few seconds later) and the rate line (mana per second as text over a dashed bar, plus a retention chip).
- Zones: `FSFiveSecondCells` (hero tile and gauge, tooltip "cells") and `FSFiveSecondRate` (rate line and chip, tooltip "rate") over the host `FSFiveSecondTab`. Display only: plain frames, no secure button, no binding, no target gate.
- Pixels: sizes and offsets are placed edge by edge on whole physical pixels (`SeatGrid`, `FT.Place`, `FT.Snap`; texels per cell pixel `n` comes from the screen height, so under 1440 px high the readout sits flush with the ring). `Crisp()` sets SetSnapToPixelGrid and SetTexelSnappingBias; the glow is its own ADD texture (`led_glow.tga`), never a blur on a segment. New TGAs need a full client restart.
- Motion: a pure function of the ledger and the clock (`Motion(now)`; `Tab.Fx()` exposes the values), no AnimationGroups. The idle glow is not on the ticker: it is looping Alpha AnimationGroups (rim ring and glow, plus one per gauge segment; `FT.breath`, `SetBreath`), stopped under the mouse, the count, a switch or a hidden tab. A per frame ticker runs only while an effect is live (`FT.fast`), a 0.05 s one (`FT.ambient`) in the regen hold and just around the kept rate stutter, 0.5 s otherwise; the ticker frame `FSFiveSecondTicker` is a child of the tab, so a hidden tab or a hidden interface (Alt+Z) has no OnUpdate at all, and the motion model gates on `IsVisible()`. Alpha animation values are in the region's own alpha space (cited in the `BreathGroup` comment); a missing rim or gauge piece logs once to the quiet degrade log. With the master off no pulse is started or counted and nothing holds the per frame ticker but the count itself. Paints are gated by a signature (`FT.sigCells`, `FT.sigRate`), so a still tab writes nothing. At most one strong motion at a time.
- Who: Priest, Mage, Warlock, Hunter, Druid, Shaman, Paladin; never Warrior or Rogue. A Druid shows it in a mana form only (`UnitPowerType`, an unreadable answer shows it). The ledger keeps listening while it is hidden.
- Seat: `ClassShoulder.lua` works the x out on every Refresh, 16 design px right of the last class tab that stands (Paladin: after the 306.8 px seal block; Shaman: after the buff tab, at 424.8), 36 when the class has no tab. A moved seat tells the tab (`changed` -> `Sync`) like a shown or hidden one. No Console, no tab.
- Ledger (the whole truth is an ESTIMATE): `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED` for `"player"` with a plain type 0 cost above 0 in `C_Spell.GetSpellPowerCost` starts five seconds; a re-cast restarts them; a cost that cannot be read plainly starts nothing; mana is never read (`UnitPower` on mana is always secret). A mana channel holds the window at the full five seconds until `UNIT_SPELLCAST_CHANNEL_STOP` (or `UnitChannelInfo` saying nothing channels), then the five seconds run. Unverified on Forever: channels, cancelled casts, free casts from procs.
- Rate: `GetPowerRegen()` is plain out of combat only. It is read once a second and at regen, kept in combat, and the RATE underline is dashed while the numbers on show are the kept ones. Casting rate shows while the window runs, the full rate otherwise. Retention is `casting / full` as a percent (cyan at 1% or more, a hollow `0%` below).
- Settings (`FS.FiveSecondTab.SETTINGS`, read by `ConfigWindow.lua`'s Unit Frames page): `fivesecond.enabled` (default on), `fivesecond.pulse` (on, also gates the rim flare), `fivesecond.sound` (off), `fivesecond.motion` (on, the master of all motion) and one switch for each effect in `FT.FX_NAMES` (`fivesecond.fx.flash`, `digits`, `wpulse`, `drop`, `sweep`, `tick`, `breath`, `glitch`, all on; the page lists them indented under the master). Each is explained by its `tip`; the client text says only "five second rule" and "regen".
- Nothing here is protected, so building, painting, the master toggle and the Druid hide are legal in combat; the host's anchor onto the Console waits for `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED` inside `Sync`. A hidden Druid form or a master toggle flipped in combat leaves the shoulder art, an EMPTY tab outline with no tiles in it, until the next out of combat Refresh.
- Guesses to probe live: the sound (`SOUNDKIT.TELL_MESSAGE`), the channel default, `UnitPowerType` being plain for a Druid, and whether `GetPowerRegen` is secret in combat outside instances.
- `tools/fivesecond-harness.py` pins it (window, pulse, fade, rate cache, chip, tooltips, settings, seats for Priest, Paladin and Shaman, secrets, combat).

## Totem presets (`FS.TotemPresets`, `FS.TotemDial`)
- Data (`TotemPresets.lua`): up to 4 presets per character in `ForeverSTUwaveDB.totemPresets[guid] = { list = { { name, picks = { element = rank 1 spell id }, order = { elements } } }, active = n, gnarly = n|nil }`. `active` may be one past the list: the NEW slot (dial shows "+", selecting it changes nothing). Names are letters, digits and spaces, up to 8, uppercased. A saved file is sanitised when it loads. A read-only Config, a database that is not a table, or a character GUID that cannot be read yet (never a made up key) keeps the presets in memory for the session and refuses edits; the module never creates or replaces the database.
- Switching: every totem of the preset the character knows is picked through `TotemBar.Pick` (so the buttons, the native multi-cast set and the Gunsight poles follow), only on a button that does not already cast it. An element the preset leaves out keeps its pick. A totem not learned yet is LOCKED (dashed grey arc, "(level N)", skipped by the step). OUT OF COMBAT ONLY: a switch in combat is queued (`P.pending`, the dial shows it amber) and applied by `PS.OnRegen`, which `TotemBar.lua` calls at `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED` inside a pcall before its own catch-up. Editing (create, rename, fill, move, delete) and the editor card are refused in combat; the card closes when combat starts.
- Cells: `TotemBar.AddCell(def)` (before the tab builds) adds a button after the six: `FSTotemButton7` is the dial (key `dial`, cyan), `FSTotemButton8` is GNARLY (key `gnarly`, pink). `TotemBar.Cols()` is 6 plus the cells that are on (a cell that is off hides and the next steps into its column); `ClassShoulder.lua` builds the shoulder per width (`colsFn`), and the buff tab and the five second rule tab follow with `after = 16`: the tab is 264 px with no cell, 306.8 with one, 349.6 with both, and the others move right with it. A world without these two files stays the six stock buttons.
- Dial: click = the step (secure `type1="macro"`, `macrotext1="/castsequence reset=combat/6 A, B, C"`, a plain `/cast A` for one totem; written in `TotemDial.ApplyCell` from the totem tab's Sync, out of combat only, rewritten only when the text changes), `shift-type1`, `alt-type1`, `alt-shift-type1`, `alt-ctrl-type1`, `alt-ctrl-shift-type1` and `ctrl-shift-type1` = "" (`EDIT_CLICKS`; a combo with no attribute of its own falls back to `type1`) so edit clicks never drop a totem, and a keybind that includes Shift edits instead of dropping (the config tip says so). The preset code reads the known totems through `PS.Known` (a per element cache of `TotemBar.KnownList`, dropped by `Dial.Refresh`, the learn events and regen), because the dial repaints ten times a second while a switch waits and every paint asks for each preset totem. Right click and the mouse wheel cycle presets (each its own setting). Shift click edits the active preset; on the NEW slot it first creates a preset as a copy of the four buttons. A press is counted on the event the game acts on (`ActionButtonUseKeyDown`).
- GNARLY (`gnarly` cell): steps ONE preset (`store.gnarly`) that a dial switch never touches, so it is armed in combat. Chosen in the settings (per character) or by USE AS GNARLY PRESET in the editor (again unties it); shift click on it edits its preset; with none tied and room under the 4 preset cap it makes an EMPTY preset (`PS.CreateGnarly`), ties it as the GNARLY preset at once and opens it, leaving the dial's active slot alone (a dial on the NEW slot stays on the NEW slot that follows; with no NEW slot left it is on preset 1, applied); with all 4 made it opens the dial's active preset (a NEW one on the NEW slot) so USE AS GNARLY PRESET can tie it; refused in combat first, and a shift click never records a press (the same blank modifier clicks as the dial, so a keybind that includes Shift edits instead of dropping, in combat too: the setting tip says to avoid it). Its script is rewritten out of combat by the totem tab's Sync, also when its preset is edited or deleted (a delete before it shifts the tie). Look: pink ring, a `!` in the middle (muted on a dashed ring with the plate UNSET when no preset is tied), press flash (`totems.gnarlyFlash`, 0.25 s) and press sound (`totems.gnarlySound`).
- Step record: our own, never read from the game. Advances on `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED` for the next totem by name, for the cell whose press came within 2 s before the cast (the game advances a sequence only when its own button runs it; a cast with no press counts for nobody, and when two cells match the later press owns it); resets at combat end, after 6 s without a press or drop (`PS.Expire`, from the dial's ticker `FSTotemDialTicker`) and on any edit or switch. A stun or failed cast can leave the game's own sequence off by one from the record until it restarts.
- Look: a ring of one arc per totem (textures `Media/Textures/totem_dial_n{n}_{i}_{kind}.tga` from `tools/assets/generate_totem_dial.py`: t quiet, g done, k next, c core, d locked, plus `totem_dial_empty.tga`) tinted by element; name plate on the rail (`totems.dialPlate`); raised plate with ">NAME" while pending or "k/n" while stepping; pending = amber dashed border, triangle, underline and amber dashed marks on the buttons that will change; applied = cyan marks for 1.6 s. The look is texture writes on plain regions only, so it is legal in combat.
- Editor: a plain card on UIParent (`FSTotemEditor`, DIALOG, closes on Escape), rows for the drop order (totem tile opens the chooser of known totems plus "left out", up and down arrows), name box (commits on Enter or focus loss), two step DELETE. The two foot buttons (DELETE, USE AS GNARLY PRESET) size to their labels: the widest label plus `D.BTN_PAD` (14, the config window's buttons) each side, never under the design width, rounded up to whole physical pixels (`SnapUp`, effective scale); the card (198 design px) grows to hold them side by side. The arrow and close buttons are fixed glyph squares. Nothing secure is anchored to it.
- Set 2: the GNARLY preset's totems are also written into the native multi-cast set 2 (Call of the Ancestors, level 30) by `PS.SyncSet2`, run from `Dial.Refresh` (so after every totem tab Sync). Out of combat only; only after every wanted element's set 1 slot is proven to map (the native `MultiCastActionButton` answers the formula id, or with none the slot holds that element's totem); set 1 is read before and after and must read the same (a changed slot is restored and set 2 is dead for the session, `set1Intact=false`), and until the sends are judged a set 1 slot that changed to the spell sent to its set 2 twin counts as the same bleed (the tab adopts its slot as the pick, so a comparison with the pick would see nothing; `set1Check=bracket|window|window+bracket|none` in the state line says which ran; any `TotemBar.Pick` (a preset switch or the picker, counted by `MC.PickSerial`) closes the window, and a set 1 slot that changed to a totem the active preset wants is counted unproven, never put back); a slot that does not read the wanted spell is sent it again after `TB.SETTLE` seconds, at most `P.SET2_ATTEMPTS` times per slot and spell (a spell that comes back later is a new attempt), and a send that did not show schedules a re-look as the picks do (`MC.ScheduleVerify`); a set 2 slot is written only when it reads back and holds nothing or a totem of its element, else it is left alone and counted `unproven=N`; unreadable set 1 slots or no Call of the Ancestors write nothing (state `wait`). Turning the setting off leaves set 2 as it is. No chat line. `totems.gnarlySet2` turns it off. Element helpers come from `TotemBar.MC` (the tab's own SendPick/ReadSlot/ActionId/NativeActionId). Set 3 (Call of the Spirits, level 40) is treated as absent.
- Settings (each with a ? tip and its own off switch, Unit Frames page group "Totem presets"): `totems.dial`, `totems.dialPlate`, `totems.dialRight`, `totems.dialWheel`, `totems.waitPulse`, `totems.waitSound` (off), `totems.appliedFlash`, `totems.appliedSound` (off), `totems.gnarly`, the GNARLY preset dropdown, `totems.gnarlyFlash`, `totems.gnarlySound` (off), `totems.gnarlySet2`.
- State line: `ForeverSTUwaveDB.totemPresetState.line` (on disk after a /reload) reports presets, active, pending, each cell's `type1`, script length, step k/n, presses and matched casts, `castids=secret` when a cast's spell id was a secret value, and `set2=<unseen|off|wait|ok n/m|partial n/m|dead (why)> [unproven=N] set1Intact=<bool> set1Check=<none|bracket|window|window+bracket>` (unproven = wanted set 2 slots that could not be judged). Never created over a missing or read-only database.
- UNVERIFIED in game (built defensively, never claimed): that a cast of the next totem within 2 s of a press from that cell is that press's cast (a press that failed, for mana or a cooldown, followed by a manual cast of the same totem advances the display though the game's own sequence did not move); that a secure `/castsequence reset=combat/6` run from the dial casts one totem per press on Forever and restarts as written; that `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED`'s spell id is plain for the player's totems; the `SOUNDKIT` names of the three cues (a client without them plays nothing); that `SetMultiCastSpell` on set 2 (set 1 action id + 4) lands and leaves set 1 alone (the state line says which); that the dial and GNARLY macros can carry the same `/castsequence` text for the same preset (two macros with identical text share the game's own sequence progress, so pressing one may move the other; our displayed record is kept apart per cell); the cell's fonts and arcs at the live scales. Needs a FULL client restart after install (new files in the toc and two new bindings), not a /reload.
- `tools/totempresets-harness.py` pins it (data, caps, sanitising, switching and the combat queue, the step script and record, the face, tooltips, the editor, the seats, the state line, secrets and hygiene); `tools/assets/generate_totem_dial.py` makes the arcs.
