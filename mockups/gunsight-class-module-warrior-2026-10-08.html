GUNSIGHT CLASS MODULE, WARRIOR

Design reference, 2026-10-08, Warrior
Three takes on the Warrior Class Module, the area option that today shows nothing for a Warrior. Each is drawn in-situ on the approved v7 target side, once in the upper area and once in the lower area, with Target Debuffs Horizontal in the other area. Target Debuffs already lists Rend, Deep Wounds, Thunder Clap, Sunder Armor and Demoralizing Shout with timers and stacks, so the module earns its place with what that list does not do: a Battle Shout self buff readout with a missing reminder, Sunder as five pips, and (variant C) a rage readout that says what you can afford now.

3 NOTES

What each variant shows

  • A, Uptime board. Battle Shout (drain bar, rebuff band, RECAST when missing), Sunder pips and count, Thunder Clap, Rend and Deep Wounds chips with time. The only variant with every named item. Largest, closest to the Paladin Seal module.
  • B, Stack and shout. Sunder pips plus count, Battle Shout time or MISSING, Thunder Clap time. Mirrors the Warlock shard and Rogue combo layout so the Class Module reads the same on every class. Smallest. Leaves bleeds to Target Debuffs.
  • C, Rage checklist. Rage bar with cost ticks plus five tiles (BS, SA, TC, RN, DW). Cyan means absent and you have the rage; amber is the Battle Shout version. Adds information no other element carries (rage vs cost), at the price of reading as a prompt rather than a status.

Why a module at all: Target Debuffs already shows Rend, Deep Wounds, Thunder Clap and Sunder x5 with timers. Battle Shout is a buff on you, so it is not in that list. Sunder as pips reads faster than a "x3" suffix on a row. Rage affordability is not shown anywhere on the Gunsight today.

My pick: B for parity and size, C if rage matters to you. A repeats too much of what the other area already lists.

Open questions

  1. Overlap: with a Warrior Class Module up, should Target Debuffs hide the spells the module owns (as it already hides Judgement for the Paladin)? The crops above show the unhidden overlap on purpose.
  2. Deep Wounds is a crit proc, not a cast, so the ledger cannot see it in combat. Drop it, or show it dashed (as drawn) as "last snapshot"?
  3. Battle Shout duplicates My buffs and the missing reminder chip under the cast bar. Keep the module row for the drain bar, or let the module carry only Sunder?
  4. Does the player's rage read plain in combat? UNVERIFIED in game. If not, C falls back to UnitPowerPercent as GunsightBoxes does, and the cost ticks would need a percent scale.
  5. Another Warrior's Battle Shout or Sunder Armor: shared auras. The snapshot sees them, the own-cast ledger does not. Is "present from anyone" the right reading for the reminder?
  6. Thunder Clap hits up to 4 targets but the debuff is tracked on the current target only. Fine for a readout, but should the chip say so in a tooltip?
  7. Cooldown (6 s) is not drawn. Worth a sweep on the TC chip, or does that clutter it?
