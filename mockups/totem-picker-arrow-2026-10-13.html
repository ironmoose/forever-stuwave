TOTEM PICKER ARROW AND ICON FLYOUT

Design reference, 2026-10-13, Shaman

Parker, 2026-10-13: the hover arrow "looks really bad, it should look like a part of the ui", and the picker should be only the totem icons with the totem's tooltip on mouse over, built from the same chrome as the tabs. Three distinct ideas, each with an arrow built into the tab and a flyout that grows out of the tab the way a shoulder grows out of the Console (one silhouette, one fill, one stroke, one halo, no seam). Same rules for all three: the arrow shows only while the cursor is over the element button or the arrow, its click area is 28 x 8.5 design px (top 0.5 under the tab's top outline, 3 px into the button face at most), nothing leaves the tab's outline while it is only an arrow, the button art is unchanged.

Level 26 Shaman: Fire (Searing, Frost Resistance, Magma), Earth (Stoneskin, Earthbind, Stoneclaw, Strength of Earth, Tremor) known, Air still a locked mold. Icon art in the flyout is a stand in: the game paints the real spell icon through the same cut seat as the tab buttons. Tooltip text is approximate, the game supplies the real GameTooltip. Dashed white rectangles in the 4x cells are the click area.
