Numbers

Read me

  • Order, top to bottom: status and cast first, buttons last. The eye lands on HP and the cast, the thing that changes, and the buttons stay a fixed strip you click, not read.
  • Cast bar speaks the player bar's language: same 18 high chevrons (9 wide, pitch 12), same icon square on the left, spell name on a tab hanging under the left end, cyan 0.2 for unlit and full cyan for lit. Timer reads "0.6 / 1.0" in the right column.
  • Idle is a ghost: a faint outline run at about a quarter alpha, no icon, no timer, no tab. It reads as an empty slot, never as a cast frozen part way (the problem the player bar had, off-state-and-bridge).
  • Low HP: fill swaps pink to hot red, name goes red, the panel outline and glow swap cyan to red and breathe (the pulse is a 1.1 s alpha loop, frozen here at one frame). It trips at 25 percent and under.
  • No pet: the whole component hides (D), matching today's ShouldShow gate. Every button is bound to a pet, so with no pet the row is dead weight, and a ghost HP bar or a ghost button row is noise next to the player's own bars. Nothing remains to collapse to; the seat stays reserved so the panel does not jump when the pet returns. A dead pet takes the same path (IsPetDead).
Recommendation: V1. A warlock pet's mana is a decision number (can the imp cast the next Firebolt), and V1 is the only one that prints it. V2 is a calmer silhouette, but its mana line is a sliver you can only read by length, and at 3 high its cut corner shrinks to 1 px. V1 costs nothing extra: same width, same height.
