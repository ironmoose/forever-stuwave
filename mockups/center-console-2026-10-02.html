Center console on the character

Parker's sketch, drawn on a desert dusk backdrop. The character stands in the middle. A cast bar plate sits at chest height on each side, a rail runs under each plate and angles down and outward. The left rail runs into the chamfered corner of the party panel, so plate, rail and panel read as one shape. The right rail carries the DoT tiles. The rotation row sits above the head. The rails are shape lines, not buttons. Panels 1 to 4 are the sketch layout at 1:1 (one UI unit is one pixel, 1480 x 720 crops of a 2560 x 1440 screen). Panel 5 is a suggested variant for comparison.
