GUNSIGHT TOT ICON

Design reference, 2026-10-09, target of target plate: a target icon, three concepts
Under the Gunsight target box the bottom edge carries the green health chip and the target of target plate (ToT). The ToT name is about to show the full name, shrinking to fit, then initials. The user wants a target icon next to it. Drawn from the real code (GunsightTags.lua): plate 69 x 15 at dx 41, seated 7 px up over the box bottom, cut2 chamfer 5 (top left and bottom right only), fill #0d0620 at .95, 1 px pink outline #ff2e97, Mononoki Bold 11 white, label width 63 (plate minus 3 px pad each side). Everything at true scale (1x) and 3x.

01 GEOMETRY AND FIT RULES USED

Health chip x 4..36 (32 wide). Gap to the plate: 5 px (x 36..41). ToT plate x 41..110 (69 wide); target box is 114 wide, so the plate stops 4 px short of the box right edge. Fit (FitTotName): the full name is measured; if it would not fit the label even at the 8 px floor it is written as initials ("Bartholomew Oldlight" becomes "B. Oldlight"); the font then shrinks from 11 toward the floor of 8 until the text fits, and anything still too wide is ellipsised. Mononoki advance is 575/1024 em = 0.5615 em per character, so a 63 px label holds 10 characters at 11 px, 11 at 10, 12 at 9 and 14 at 8. Shrink is assumed to step in whole pixels. An icon inside the plate takes label width, which is the argument for widening the plate; widening grows it to the right (seat unchanged), past the box right edge by the amount noted in each card.

03 THREE ICON CONCEPTS

Three different ideas, not recolours: a glyph inside the plate (A), a pointer outside the plate that costs no label width (B), and an inverted tab cell fused onto the plate edge (C). Each shows "Sneaky Toots" and the long "Bartholomew Oldlight" through the same fit rules, so the shrink and the initials fallback are visible.
