SHAMAN BUFF TAB

Design reference, 2026-10-12, Shaman

The IMBUE and SHIELD self reminders leave the Gunsight area and become a second shoulder tab on the Console's top edge, immediately right of the totem tab. Same chrome as the totem tab (the Console's own fill, stroke and halo, top left cut 6, right foot flaring 10), same 38 px buttons. Two clickable tiles: IMBUE casts the imbue, SHIELD casts Lightning Shield. States and colours are the approved Gunsight chips (quiet gold, amber hollow when missing, hollow ? when unreadable), plus pink for expiring. Everything at 1 design px = 1 px unless a caption says 2x.

A // CONSOLE TOP EDGE, REAL SCALE

Cropped strip of the Console (chassis left to chassis right, the lower rows fade out). Level 14 Shaman: Earth and Fire totems learned, Water, Air, Set drop and Recall still locked molds. The empty top edge between the new tab and the HUD key tab stays empty.

B // TILE STATE SHEET shown at 2x, one 38 px tile on the Console fill

IMBUE tilechip = imbue code (RB Rockbiter, FT Flametongue, FB Frostbrand, WF Windfury), text = time left on the main hand
SHIELD tilechip = LS, text = orb count (Lightning Shield: 3 orbs, 10 min)

C // THE TAB AT 2x, WITH HOVER

IMBUE is pinned in its hover state with the tooltip it shows. This zoom is live: hover either tile to see the same (cyan border, tooltip above). Hover is the only feedback a click needs, the cast itself shows on the tile within a tick (the chip goes quiet when the buff lands).

D // TOOLTIPS

Same anchor and tone as the totem buttons (title in the tile colour, one plain line of state, one line for the click). The locked line is the totem tab's own: "Opens at level N."

E // SPEC

GEOMETRY (DESIGN PX)

Block92.8 x 49: 2 columns x 1 row of the shoulder block (padding 6, button 38, gap 4.8, top 6, bottom 5), the totem tab's numbers with COLS = 2. Same height as the totem tab, so the two top edges line up.
SeatBOTTOMLEFT on the Console TOPLEFT at x = 316. The totem tab ends at 300 and its foot flare reaches 310, so 6 px of chassis top line stays visible between the two shoulders, then the new tab's own straight left side and 6 px top left cut.
ChromePetDock.NewShoulder with opts.chassis, exactly like ClassShoulder: Console fill top stop, stroke alpha .72, the Console's own baked halo, bottom open, fill 1.2 into the chassis. The Console outline is open under it (its own gap span, DX 316 to 418.8).
Tiles38 x 38, top left and bottom right chamfer 5 (the totem mold's shape), 1 px border violet at .35, 3 px underline in the state colour. Chip 22 x 15 on top, text 11 px below.

STATES AND DATA

Quiet (on)Gold chip, text at 85%. Imbue over 5 min, shield over 5 min.
ExpiringPink chip, text, border and underline. Under 5 min on the clock; minutes become seconds under 1 min. Shield shows its time while expiring and the orb count otherwise.
Missing (warn)Amber hollow chip, border and underline glow, the word IMBUE or SHIELD in amber.
UnreadableHollow muted chip and a ? when the data cannot be read in combat. Imbue lasts 60 min so this mostly shows for the shield.
EstimateShield orbs in combat from our own hit ledger: "~3", dashed underline.
Not learnedLocked mold with the unlock level, dimmed to 40%, never hidden (Show and Hide on a secure button are refused in combat). SHIELD is L8. IMBUE never locks (Rockbiter is level 1).
ClickSecure spell button per tile, one attribute written out of combat, like the totem Set drop and Recall buttons. IMBUE casts the imbue its chip shows, or the best known one when none is up. SHIELD casts Lightning Shield.
