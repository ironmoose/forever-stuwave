GUNSIGHT CLASS MODULE, HUNTER

Design reference, 2026-10-08, Hunter
The Hunter Class Module does not copy the Paladin Seal shape. Three divergent concepts were drawn from what a Forever Hunter really does: stand still for the last half second of every Auto Shot, stay out of the dead zone, keep your shots and stings rolling. Each is drawn in-situ on the approved v7 target side (here in the lower area, Target Debuffs Horizontal above). One concept is dropped, one is pushed further, and the best idea from each of the others is folded into the pick. Research: adze doc 01M4F7MDKTAA2F3CX1GB8J4RH9.

5 NOTES

Why DRAW and not the other two

  • It is the only concept whose picture IS the mechanic. A Forever Auto Shot is a draw: the timer runs, then you stop for the last 0.5 s, then it releases. The string pulling back, the amber full draw and the release are the Auto Shot cycle, not a gauge that happens to be about it.
  • It carries the whole Hunter problem in one silhouette. Bow = shot timing. Bullseye sliding along the flight lane = melee / dead zone / shot range. Venom ring and mark ticks on the bullseye = Serpent Sting and Hunter's Mark. Two quiver tiles = the two real cooldown groups (Aimed + Multi, Arcane + Hawk) and ammo.
  • Nothing else in the HUD is a bow. The Paladin is a drain bar, the Warlock shards, the Rogue pips. LOCK reads as a radial cooldown with a crosshair, which every shooter HUD has.
  • Taken from LOCK: the blind dead zone (red hatch) is now the red hatch in the flight lane and the red bow. Taken from PACK: for a Beast Mastery Hunter the ARC tile turns into HWK with two hawk pips (Summon Hawk shares Arcane Shot's cooldown, max two hawks).

Open questions

  1. Is the 0.5 s wind-up in the live build? Blizzard says the near zero wind-up is a bug (2026-09-23) and the Oct 1 and Oct 8 notes do not list a fix. If it ships as is, HOLD collapses to nothing and the module is a pure "AIM NOW" cue. Is that acceptable, or should HOLD stay as a 0.5 s hint anyway?
  2. AIM NOW assumes Aimed Shot (2.0 s cast) started with less than 2.0 s to the next shot delays Auto Shot, as in Classic. UNVERIFIED on Forever. If Forever lets Auto Shot fire during the cast (early beta reports say so), the AIM zone should become "any time".
  3. Melee swings reset the ranged timer (Wrath rule, Blizzard says intended, another post says bug). The module cannot see a melee swing in combat, so the draw will be wrong until the next shot resyncs it. Show nothing special, or fade the bow for 1 s after a melee ability?
  4. The caption chooses between AIM NOW, ARC READY and DRAWING. Is that too close to a rotation prompt for the module, which today only reports state?
  5. Config: one Class Module, no option? A "show dead zone lane" toggle for Survival players who live in melee?
