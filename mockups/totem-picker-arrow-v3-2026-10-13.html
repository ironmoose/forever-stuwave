TOTEM PICKER ARROW V3

Parker's design, 2026-10-13, Shaman

Parker's whiteboard sketch, built to the real button geometry. On hover a separate flat piece appears above the element button, styled like the button (same fill, same element coloured stroke as the button's hover look). Its lower left edge runs parallel to the button's chamfer cut with a thin even seam, so the cut seems to continue as a line. The piece is a thick wedge over the cut corner plus a thinner bar running right along the top of the button, like a folder tab. A synthwave neon arrow points up and to the right, along the chamfer direction, inside the wedge end.

The height conflict, both shown. At sketch proportions the piece is about 11 px tall on the 38 px button, but the gap between the button top and the tab's top rail is only 6 px. The earlier rule (arrow stays inside the tab outline) is overridden by the sketch, so V1 "As drawn" follows the sketch and the tab outline grows around it while hovered. V2 "Fits the gap" squeezes the same shape under the rail. Parker judges.

The arrow style (both variants, all states): a neon double line. An outer blurred haze and a gradient body run from the element colour at the tail to hot magenta at the head, with a thin near white core on top, and the piece carries faint scanlines behind it. Dim on button hover, full glow on arrow hover, flipped to point down and left when the list is open. The flyout is concept A's, redrawn unchanged. Level 26 Shaman: Fire and Earth known, Air still locked. Icon art in the flyout is a stand in and the tooltip text is approximate. Dashed white rectangles in the 4x cells are the click area.
