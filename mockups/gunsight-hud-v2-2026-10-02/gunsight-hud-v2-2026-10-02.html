  • YOUR CAST (1)Left tape, like an airspeed tape. Chevrons fill upward and the hollow caret sits at true progress. The box on its inner side names the spell and the timer, and a small icon square of the spell sits beside the box (dimmed when the cast is interrupted).
  • NEXT CAST (2)The spell the profile says to cast next, left of your info box, as a tile showing the spell's own icon (Shadow Bolt here), seated the way the action buttons seat theirs: cut at the top left and bottom right, the square icon inset so its corners hide under the cut. Cyan border, gold while a proc makes it the move. The small number in the corner is its keybind.
  • SOUL SHARDS (3)Under your tape, right aligned to it. One line art soul shard per held shard (4 here; the real UI draws at most 4 and shows a plus N above that): an outline of an elongated faceted crystal with three interior facet lines in violet with the glow, and a faint violet fill on the bright top facet. Each is 10 x 18 units with 4 between. Only held shards are drawn, no empty slots.
  • BUFF REMINDER (4)A 24 unit tile under the shards when a self buff is missing (Demon Armor here, shown with its icon). The border pulses pink until you recast it.
  • SPELL ROWReplaced. The DoT time tape covers DoT timers and the next-cast tile covers what to cast.
  • TARGET CAST (5)Right tape, mirrored, in hot pink so it never reads as yours. The fill is timed by the engine as one rising mask, with no caret and no timer. Name and spell are separate fields. A small icon square of the spell sits beside the box (Fear here; Mending is not a warlock spell, so it shows a neutral placeholder). A KICK tag and a pulsing outline mean you can interrupt. Grey fill and a padlock mean you cannot.
  • TARGET BARSA small health and power gauge beside the target name, on the Target bars switch (A Stacked slim, B Underline, C Edge gauge, Off for today's box). Health is the party row look (hostile green, red at 35% or less); power is coloured by type: mana cyan, rage red, energy gold, focus orange. Bars are plain fill fractions, so no number is read or drawn. The box grows only for the option shown. Preview with the Target state switch or #tgtbars=a|b|c|off and #tgtstate=full|mid|low|rage|energy|focus|none.
  • DOT TIMERS (6)This slot is class specific: a Warlock gets the DoT time tape described here, a Priest gets the same tape with three lanes (Shadow Word: Pain, Vampiric Touch, Devouring Plague), a Paladin gets the Seal Chamber below, and all sit behind key 6. An altitude scale from 0 to 30 seconds. Each DoT is a square chip, cut at the top left and bottom right, filled edge to edge by its spell icon (Corruption, Curse of Agony, Immolate, Siphon Life) with the border over it, that slides down toward the amber refresh band, like notes on a rhythm game highway. Recast when it lands. Remaining time per DoT is unverified on this client (pending probe). A hollow chip at 0 with a dim grey icon means that DoT is not on the target.
  • SEAL CHAMBER (6)The Paladin module in the DoT slot (Class switch, Paladin). A chamfered chamber 164 wide, its left edge the 0 to 30 second ruler, so the time scale is the same one the DoT tape uses. The active seal is a line art sigil inside a ring lens with three bright arcs turning slowly, its name above and the seconds left below, in the seal's colour: Righteousness gold (scales), Crusader orange (crossed swords), Fury red (flame), Command purple (crown), Light white (sun), Wisdom blue (eye), Justice silver (gavel). The 30 second duration is a drain tube beside the ruler, filled bottom to top like the DoT rails and draining down. At 5 seconds or less the whole chamber flickers like a neon tube about to die (dropouts get likelier and deeper toward 0), and the amber refresh band across the foot is the reseal cue: at 3 seconds or less it lights and its RESEAL label flashes, and the tube turns amber. NO SEAL in combat: the chamber goes dark, with red scanlines, a red scan sweeping down it and a pulsing red NO SEAL label (the same pulse as the buff reminder), so the Buff Reminder piece is hidden for a Paladin rather than say the same thing twice. JUDGEMENT: casting it sends a ring out of the sigil and a comet to the target info box, which flashes in the seal's colour. Judgement of Light, Wisdom, the Crusader and Justice also leave a debuff, shown as a chip in the 40 second lane beside the chamber (Justice lasts 10 seconds); Righteousness, Command and Fury leave none, so their Judgement shows the pulse only and the lane says NO DEBUFF. Judgement does not consume the seal on Forever. Preview with the Seal state switch (Cycle runs active, expiring, judged and no seal, and rotates the seal; the other four hold one look) and the Seal switch, or #class=paladin, #sealstate=cycle|active|expiring|judged|noseal and #seal=righteousness|crusader|fury|command|light|wisdom|justice. Add &t=0.7 with #sealstate=judged to freeze the pulse in flight. Cycle runs the 30 second seal about 2.3 times faster than real time so each state shows in one pass. Other Paladin swaps: Soul Shards hidden, the proc rungs become Holy Strike (left) and Judgement (right), lit while ready (off cooldown), with their icons, and the next cast tile shows the Holy Strike icon.
  • CLASS SWITCHWarlock (the approved look, unchanged), Warrior, Paladin or Priest, on the Class switch or #class=warlock|warrior|paladin|priest; the choice is remembered. The DoT slot (key 6) is the class module seat: the DoT time tape for a Warlock and for a Priest, the Seal Chamber for a Paladin, and for a Warrior an empty dashed box labelled CLASS MODULE TBD, because no Warrior module has been designed. Only the Warlock has a pet panel (the Priest's Shadowfiend borrows it, see PRIEST), and the bars of the other classes dock into the Console as shoulders (STANCE BAR, AURA BAR AND SEAL BAR, PRIEST). Every spell tile shows the real icon from the client. A Warrior hides the Soul Shards, shows Battle Shout missing in the Buff Reminder, relabels the proc posts to Overpower on the left and Revenge on the right, shows Overpower in the Next Cast tile, casts Slam in the cast box, and the player's own party row takes the class colour with a red rage rail instead of mana. A Paladin casts Holy Light and shows Holy Strike in the Next Cast tile. A Priest is described under PRIEST.
  • STANCE BAR (Warrior)The stance bar (StanceBar.lua) docked as a left shoulder of the Console chassis, standing on its top edge at x 755 of 2560 (24 clear of the chat terminal's outer edge) and built like the pet panel's Console-docked look: a straight left side, the top left cut at 6, a right foot that flares out at 45 degrees, open into the chassis, so the chassis outline, its violet rail and its halo run over it as one line with no seam. Three action buttons (38 design px, 4.8 apart, 6 of padding): Battle, Defensive and Berserker, each with its real stance icon on the action button plate (cut at the top left and bottom right, a cyan border with a faint halo). The active stance has the cyan checked wash and a brighter ring; pick it on the Stance switch or #stance=battle|defensive|berserker. A Warlock has no forms, so it has no shoulder. In the Bar-matched and Deck panel styles the same shoulder is drawn live on top of the stack instead. Changing the stance runs the same neon ring as the Console keys: the new stance button ignites (a tube flicker around its edge, then a steady glow that fades) while the old one browns out toward red, stutters, cuts and fades over about a second, still showing its lit look until it goes dark; the page opening, a class switch and reduced motion just swap the lit button.
  • AURA BAR AND SEAL BAR (Paladin)One tall docked shoulder (306.8 wide, 92 high, the same open shape as the stance shoulder) holding both rows: the seal bar on top and the aura bar against the chassis, 7 buttons each, 38 design px and 4.8 apart, with real icons. Tall rather than stepped because both rows are the same width, so a step would only add a pair of corners; one block keeps the plain shoulder shape the pet panel and the HUD key tab already have. Paladin auras are shapeshift forms on a TBC era client (unverified on Forever): Devotion, Retribution, Concentration, the Shadow, Frost and Fire resistance auras and Sanctity (the eighth, Crusader Aura, is left out to keep the row at 7). The active aura takes the checked look and follows the Aura switch or #aura=devotion|retribution|concentration|none (None checks no button); the checked button is the only sign of the active aura, nothing is drawn around the console. The seals, in the order of the chamber (Righteousness, Crusader, Fury, Command, Light, Wisdom, Justice), keep a border in their own colour; the active seal is lit with that colour as border and wash, and its 30 second drain runs as a thin underline inside the button (amber at 3 seconds or less, the reseal band). With no seal nothing is lit. A change of aura or of seal runs the same ignite and outage ring as the Console keys, one row at a time: the new button strikes up in its colour (cyan for an aura, the seal's own colour for a seal) while the old one browns out, stutters and cuts out over about a second, holding its lit look until it is dark; None and No seal give only the outage. The Cycle state rotates the seal by itself and animates the same way (the old seal goes out when its 13 seconds end, the next one lights 4 seconds later, so the two never overlap); the page opening, a class switch and reduced motion just swap the lit button.
  • PRIESTClass switch, Priest, or #class=priest. Key 6 is the DoT time tape with Shadow Word: Pain (18 s), Vampiric Touch (15 s) and Devouring Plague (24 s) in three lanes, the real icons on the chips. Soul Shards are hidden; the Buff Reminder is Inner Fire missing; the Next Cast tile is Mind Blast; the cast box is Mind Flay, drawn as a plain cast because the tape has no channel mode yet (in game it is a 3 second channel). The profile has no Priest procs, so the two rungs are cooldown ready cues rather than procs: Mind Blast on the left (lit while ready) and Shadowfiend on the right (lit while it is off cooldown). Shadowform is a one button stance shoulder (always on) at the left seat. The Shadowfiend (15 s out, 5 minute cooldown) takes the seat to its right so the pet panel never fights Shadowform for the left seat: while it is away, a one button cooldown tile (gold ring when ready, the pet bar swipe over the 5 minutes with m:ss left when on cooldown); while it is out, the tile opens into the pet panel (the Console-docked shape, with its name, level, HP and mana, a 10 slot bar of Attack, Follow, Stay and the three modes, and in the cast area a TIME LEFT run that drains over its 15 seconds and turns amber in the last 3). Preview on the Shadowfiend switch or #fiend=cycle|ready|out|cooldown (the cooldown runs 10 times faster here so a cycle fits in under a minute; the panel numbers are placeholders). The player's own party row takes the Priest white.
  • SECRETSTarget cast timing is hidden from addons, so the target tape is an engine-timed fill with no caret or timer. Name and spell are separate fields.
  • PROCS (7)A post on the inner side of each tape, centred on the horizon. Trance on the left, Backlash on the right. Hidden until the proc is active: an idle proc shows nothing at all, no post, no dim label, no icon. When the proc goes active the whole rung fades in linearly over 0.3 s: the dashed guide, a solid amber post that grows from the horizon, and the label between the info box and the tape with the proc's icon beside it. Preview a lit proc with the Procs state switch or #procs=cycle|trance|backlash|both|idle (Cycle, the default, lights each one in turn). Other classes relabel the posts and take their own icons (see CLASS SWITCH and PRIEST).
  • ACTION BARSThe real stack, drawn from geometry measured in game and fixed to the screen like the party frame, so the camera zoom never moves it. Three rows of two bars, twelve buttons each. In the default Console style the whole stack is ONE console chassis: a single cut-corner silhouette with a violet rail, the buttons set straight on the chassis fill, a colour pip beside each row on the left edge (the pink pip marks MAIN, row 1 on the left), a spine between the left and right halves, a hairline between rows with a tick ruler under each row, a screw at the shoulder's top right and another at the bottom left, and a faint serial along the foot. Buttons are cyan edged cut plates with the spell icon inset and the keybind top right. Empty plates are unbound or empty slots. The Panel style switch also offers Bar-matched and Deck, which draw the stack as six separate dark cut-corner pills with a violet edge and the MAIN bar (top left) in pink with a MAIN tag. The class bars dock on the chassis top edge as shoulders at the left (see STANCE BAR, AURA BAR AND SEAL BAR and PRIEST).
  • PARTY FRAME (8)The real party frame, drawn at its seat and size (231 x 294 design px): five rows (you first, then a dead priest, an offline mage, a hunter with a cleansable Poison debuff and its pet, a low health warrior), the 24 wide pet column and the alert tray outside the left edge (the right edge is where the left cast tape sits). The laser rails are the approved ones and are drawn exactly as they are now (class colour health over power colour mana, 9 tall cores, 4 bloom, 16 spark, 0.35 tail to 1 tip, 4 apart, the dispel halo around the pair). Only the chrome around them changes, on the Party style switch: Proposed (see below); Console-matched puts the rows on one chassis like the action bar console (cut top left and bottom right, violet rail and outside halo, a pip per row, a hairline and tick ruler under each row, a spine to the pet column, two screws and a serial); Box-matched gives each member its own Gunsight info box (the name as the header word, cut top left and bottom right, cyan edge, steel dashed when dead or offline, red when low, the dispel colour on a cleansable debuff); Bracket HUD has no panel at all, just bracket arms in the four corners, hairline dividers and a tape ruler beside each row. Current is the old dashed ghost, kept to compare. Proposed, the default, is the one meant for the real UI. It has no filled panel, only the Bracket HUD language: bracket arms at the four corners and hairline dividers between rows, the same line look as the Gunsight tapes. The frame is sized to the members actually present (the Members control, 1 to 5, top anchored, so one member is one row high instead of a five row box). The solid pet block is gone: each pet gets a thin vertical laser rail in the pet colour (the locked rail recipe turned on its side, with a dim track, a gradient core, a small bloom and a tip spark), and a member without a pet has no pet column at all. The frame sits 24 design px left of the left cast tape (never closer than 16, brackets included) and the alert tray stays outside its left edge. MISSING BUFF (the Missing buff control, shown as a priest would see it): a buff you can cast that a member lacks joins the alert tray after any cleansable debuff, as the spell's own icon. Missing is a red ring around the spell's icon tinted dim red (the Lua's .55 / .12 / .12 at 55% alpha), here Fortitude on Tarsk (left of his Poison) and Brannoch; a buff that is up never alerts, and the dead priest and the offline mage get none, since they cannot be buffed. Hover in game says Missing: Power Word: Fortitude. HOT COUNTDOWN (the HoT countdown control, shown as a priest would see it): your own Renew on a member, with the seconds left counting down on the same clock as the DoT chips, here 11 on Tarsk and 3 on Brannoch while you, the dead priest and the offline mage show none, so absence reads at a glance. It sits on top of any party style, in one of four looks. Tray tile, the default: a 22 chamfered tile in the alert tray after the cleansable debuff and the missing buffs, the real Renew icon with a radial swipe over the elapsed part and the seconds on top. Header tile: a small 17 by 12 tile in the row header, left of the LOW tag or the LV text, the seconds in it. Timer bar: a 3 tall bar under the rail pair inside the box, its fill draining from the left toward the seconds at its right end. Rail text: only the seconds, a small mono tag over the right end of the HP rail. In all four looks the HoT turns amber at 3 seconds or less, the same refresh band the DoT time tape uses, the cue to recast. Key 8 shows or hides it. In the real UI solo (no group) hides the frame entirely, since your own unit frame already shows you.
  • PET (9)The pet frame, pet cast bar and pet action bar as ONE chamfered panel (PetFrame.lua, PetCastBar.lua, PetActionBar.lua), seated where Layout.petcontainer puts it: 348 design px wide, bottom edge 8 above the Console halo (CONSOLE_PET_GAP), left edge 12 right of the chat terminal. Top row: name and level, then HP over mana as laser rails on the left 2/5 (134 px), the chevron cast bar on the right 3/5 (198 px, Firebolt here, spell icon, timer). Under it the 10 slot bar, 30 px buttons 4 apart: Attack (pink flash while the pet attacks), Follow (cyan mode check), Firebolt (pulsing green autocast ring, gold edge while it casts), Fire Shield (faint ring, autocast allowed but off), Phase Shift (cooldown swipe with seconds), then Aggressive, Defensive (the second cyan mode check) and Passive, with the keybind top right. The Pet switch has three looks. A Brief (default): exactly the brief, 82 high, cyan outline. B Box-matched: the rails and the cast bar each sit in a party style row box with a header band (tile, spaced name, LV, a LOW tag), 88 high, violet frame so the boxes carry the cyan. C Console-docked: a slim 68 high shoulder standing on the Console's top edge at the left, open into the chassis like the HUD key tab is on the right (a 26 high top row, the cast name over the timer, its chevron run at the standard chevron size, 7 arm, 14 high, 10 pitch, filling the track from the icon to the timer); the 2 x 5 fold was weighed and dropped, it is 64 high on its own and leaves a 170 wide column. Pet state cycles casting, idle (an empty dim track, no icon, no timer) and low HP (red edge that pulses, red name and fill) by itself, or pick one. In game the autocast ring is steady lit; it breathes here so a still frame shows it.
  • CONTROL DECKIn the real UI a mini Control Deck docks on the right top bar. In the default Console style it is the stepped shoulder of the chassis: the outline steps up at 45 degrees over the right half and the tab's right side runs on as the chassis edge, one line with no seam, with its keys drawn as action buttons (the part's colour only on the glyph and the LED underline). The other two styles are on the Panel style switch: Bar-matched is a tab grown out of the top right pill, and Deck is the full trapezoid chassis of the real deck (slanted shoulders, cyan left and pink right, neon top rail with a glint) with a coloured key per part. Eight glyph keys cut at the top left and bottom right, one per HUD part, in three groups: Your Cast (1), Next Cast (2), Soul Shards (3) and Buff Reminder (4) on your side, Target Cast (5), DoT Timers (6) and Procs (7) on the enemy side, then Party Frame (8) on its own. Hover or focus a key for a tooltip with its name and key number, and press Escape to hide it. Turning a part on strikes its neon ring up with the pet autocast ignite flicker; turning it off runs the power outage (brownout toward red, stutter, cut, fade, about 1 s). In Deck the rail segment above each key lights in the key's colour while its part is on and dims when it is off, following the ring; in the other two styles the LED underline inside the key does the same. Each part fades on its own, so YOU no longer hides the next cast tile, the shards or the buff reminder. Keys 1 to 8 work too. The Panel style control switches the three live and remembers the choice. The dashed party box (the Current party style) is a placement guide, not part of the HUD.
  • HORIZONOne hairline at chest height ties every scale to the same 50% line. It breaks around your character.
  • KEEP-OUTThe ellipse around your character and the ground at your feet stays empty. The strip above the head stays empty too.
