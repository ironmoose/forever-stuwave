GUNSIGHT CLASS MODULE, PRIEST

Design reference, 2026-10-08, Priest
The Class Module area for a Priest at the beta level cap of 30, drawn in the approved v7 chrome. Target Debuffs already shows Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague on the target, so this module only carries what that list cannot: your own upkeep (Inner Fire charges, Fortitude), shield and cooldown timing, and the Shadow Weaving and Spirit Tap windows. Three variants, A to C. Each row shows the module in the lower area (full scene, the v7 default seat), in the upper area (target side crop), and a 1.5x annotated copy with its data paths. Shadowform (a 31 point talent) and Shadow Word: Death (level 32) are above the cap and are not drawn.
