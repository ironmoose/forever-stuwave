GUNSIGHT CLASS MODULE, SHAMAN

Design reference, 2026-10-08, Shaman
The Class Module area for a Shaman at the beta level cap of 30, in the approved v7 chrome. The Paladin Seal module is the quality bar (instantly readable, tells you when to act), not the shape. Three concepts were drawn to differ in form and metaphor: A, Totem Poles (pillars that sink into a ground line, shock charging along the ground), B, Totem Field (a top down range radar around your placed totems) and C, Spirit Compass (an elemental ring, dropped, sketch at the bottom).
