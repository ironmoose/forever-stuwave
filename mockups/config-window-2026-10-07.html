Taken from the addon
  • Panel: SkinPanel chrome. Violet 1px ring and glow, fill #1a1025 at 92%, cut corners top left and bottom right (6), scanline at .25, 18 high title band with a rule and the mono stuwave://config label.
  • Well: BugReport's #0a0416 at 95% reading area with a 4 wide violet scroll thumb. Close X: SkinCloseButton, pink mono X in a pink cut ring.
  • Type: FS Display for the title, Mononoki Bold for everything else, cyan for live text, muted #9d93c4 for helpers.
The ? icons carry the explanations; hover one in the browser to read it, A shows one open. Focus, pressed and disabled states are not drawn. A and the two thumbnails are the same window, so the nav and footer never move between categories.
