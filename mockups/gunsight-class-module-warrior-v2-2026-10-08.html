GUNSIGHT CLASS MODULE, WARRIOR v2

Design reference, 2026-10-08, Warrior v2
Round 1 was a list of timers; the first take of v2 copied the Paladin shape (gauge, band, lane). Both are out. The Paladin module is the quality bar, not the layout, and each class module has to look like its own class. This sheet explores three divergent Warrior concepts with their own form factor and motif, self-critiques them, drops the weakest and pushes the best (a faceted greatsword) all the way through: in-situ scene, anatomy, states, data path. The second best (a war drum) is kept as the alternative. All drawn on the v7 target side, one area (202 x 128), Target Debuffs in the other.
