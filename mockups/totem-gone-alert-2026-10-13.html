TOTEM GONE ALERT

Design reference, 2026-10-13, Shaman

A Shaman player cannot tell mid fight whether her Fire totem (her AoE) is still up. Today the only cue is the totem button's cooldown sweep quietly clearing on the Console totem tab, and she misses it. Decided with Parker: the alert covers all four totems (Fire, Earth, Water, Air), and a totem going away is one state, "gone", whether it was killed or simply ran out. It must be clearly noticeable, and every part of it is optional (section D).

Three distinct concepts, each drawn the same way: one totem gone (Magma, in its element colour), two gone at once (Magma and Healing Stream), then a timeline of how it fades out and what clears it (re-dropping the totem), and a 2x close up. Scenes are 1x design px: a game strip with the Gunsight centre (player, cast bar ticks, party and target frames) and the top of the Console with the totem tab, level 30 Shaman, all four totems learned. The totem art is a stand in for the spell icon. Sound is optional in every concept and never the only cue.

D // SETTINGS ROW

Shaman settings, Totems group. Remind me about is ONE shared list (Fire, Earth, Water, Air) that drives both surfaces: the on screen Alert and the Gunsight Next Cast tile, which will suggest dropping a gone totem again. Below it each surface has its own on/off toggle, and the Alert has its own parts (sound, flash and glow) that switch off on their own. Everything is optional, nothing is forced. The Next Cast tile itself is not drawn here: it is out of scope for this mockup (a separate task, and there is no Shaman Next Cast profile yet). Explanations live in the circled ? tooltip, there is no helper text under any row. Left: the example state, only Fire ticked, both surfaces on, tooltip open. Right: the Alert off while Next Cast stays on (its parts dim and keep their values).

E // BEHAVIOUR AND PICK

BEHAVIOUR (ALL THREE)

  • No alert for a deliberate dismissal. Totemic Recall and Shift right click dismiss all (the totem bar's own dismiss) clear the totems without any alert and clear alerts already showing.
  • Re-dropping the same element replaces the totem and must not flash. The old totem going away because the new one landed is not a "gone", so nothing fires, and any alert for that element clears at once.
  • One state. Gone is gone: killed and run out look and sound the same, in the element colour. No timer test is needed.
  • One shared list. Remind me about (Fire, Earth, Water, Air) drives the Alert and the Next Cast tile together. An element left off gets no alert and no suggestion; the other elements still do.
  • Per element slot. Each element has one slot, so a new alert for an element replaces that element's older alert.
  • Only totems that were up. An element that was never dropped, or is not learned yet, never alerts.
  • Every part optional. Alert on/off, Next Cast on/off, Play sound, Flash and glow, and each element in the list switch off on their own. With Flash and glow off: A loses its pulse rings, B stops blinking and keeps a steady border, C loses the edge glow and keeps the banner.
  • Sound is optional. If Play sound is on, one short Blizzard sound (PlaySound, a UI sound kit) plays when the alert first shows. Off means silent, nothing else changes.

HOW THE THREE COMPARE

A calloutWhere she is already looking (above the player at the Gunsight), so it is the hardest to miss mid fight. It covers part of the view for about 6 s, and it is gone after that, so a missed one is lost.
B tab flareCosts no screen space and stays until she re-drops, so it can never be lost, but it sits at the bottom of the screen, the place she already misses it. Best as the persistent half of a pair.
C bannerLoudest by area, with the edge glow reaching the corners of her vision. Most intrusive in a long fight with several totems running out, and the banner shape is new chrome.
PickA as the alert, B as the free backup that stays. C only if A turns out too small in real play.
