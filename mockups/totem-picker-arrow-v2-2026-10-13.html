TOTEM PICKER ARROW V2

Design reference, 2026-10-13, Shaman

Parker on concept A (latch): "I do like the menu up though that can stay. just the arrow... it still looks detached from the ui, it needs something." The flyout below is A's, redrawn unchanged in every LIST OPEN state. Only the arrow (the hover affordance on the element button, and its close state while the list is open) is new.

Why A's arrow reads detached: it is a small separate tongue with its own fill and its own stroke, floating in the 6 px gap between the button and the tab's top rail with dark space around it, so it looks like a sticker on the UI. When the list is open the chevron is a loose piece wedged between flyout and button. Fix used here: the arrow is made from an outline or surface that already exists (the button's own border, the lit gap between button and rail, the button's own chamfer cut), joined to what is around it on every side. Nothing floats, nothing has a gap around it.

Rules kept from Parker's earlier decisions, all three concepts obey them: the arrow shows only while the cursor is on the element button or the arrow; the arrow and its click area stay inside the tab outline at rest and on hover (nothing pokes above the tab's top rail until the flyout is open); the click area goes at most 3 px into the button face; the button art at rest is untouched; Alt-click still opens the picker. A rail bump that rose above the tab was drawn first and dropped for breaking the inside rule.

Level 26 Shaman: Fire (Searing, Frost Resistance, Magma), Earth (Stoneskin, Earthbind, Stoneclaw, Strength of Earth, Tremor) known, Air still a locked mold. Icon art in the flyout is a stand in: the game paints the real spell icon through the same cut seat as the tab buttons. Tooltip text is approximate, the game supplies the real GameTooltip. Dashed white rectangles in the 4x cells are the click area.
